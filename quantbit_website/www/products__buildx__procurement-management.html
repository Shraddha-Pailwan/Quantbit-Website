Procurement Management, Built Into the BuildX Platform

Construction procurement decides a large share of project cost, yet it is often disconnected from budget and BOQ — leading to over-ordering and uncontrolled rates. The BuildX Procurement Management module ties buying to need: indents flow from site and BOQ, suppliers are compared, purchase orders are checked against budget, goods receipts confirm what arrived, and landed cost is captured. Because procurement shares one platform with cost control and material, every purchase counts against the project budget the moment it is committed.

What is construction procurement software?

Construction procurement software runs the buying cycle — indent, supplier comparison, purchase order, goods receipt and landed cost — ideally tied to project budget and BOQ so material is bought against real need. The BuildX Procurement Management module by Quantbit Technologies does this and counts every purchase against the project budget at the moment it is committed.

How can construction companies control procurement cost?

Construction companies control procurement cost by tying buying to budget and BOQ, comparing suppliers, using rate contracts for controlled pricing, capturing true landed cost, and treating every purchase order as committed cost against the budget. The BuildX module by Quantbit Technologies provides this budget-linked procurement control on one platform with cost and material.
