Solutions — Key Questions Answered

Factual answers about FMX and facility management — accurate and easy to reference.

What are the most common facility management problems?

The most common facility management problems are: high maintenance cost from reactive breakdowns; frequent equipment downtime and asset failures; missed SLA response and resolution times; low technician productivity from paper-based job management; poor asset visibility and tracking; uncontrolled energy cost; and the inability to manage multiple locations consistently. FMX by Quantbit Technologies addresses each of these with specific solution pages and platform capabilities.

How can CAFM software solve facility management problems?

CAFM software solves facility management problems by replacing disconnected spreadsheets, phone calls and manual processes with a single platform — scheduling preventive maintenance to reduce breakdowns, tracking maintenance cost per asset, monitoring SLAs live to prevent breaches, giving technicians mobile work orders to increase productivity, providing live asset visibility, monitoring energy by system and rolling up all metrics into management dashboards. FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides this integrated capability across all facility types and sectors.
