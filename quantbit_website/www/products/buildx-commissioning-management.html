What is commissioning management in construction?

Commissioning management is the disciplined close-out of a construction or EPC project — verifying each system through testing and checklists, driving the punch list to closure, and confirming handover readiness with documentation. It addresses the final 5% of a project that causes a disproportionate share of delays. The BuildX Commissioning Management module by Quantbit Technologies manages this system by system on one platform with cost and progress.
