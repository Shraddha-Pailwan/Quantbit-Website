The problem

An organisation with facilities in many locations — branches, campuses, plants, stores — usually runs each on its own spreadsheets and its own ad-hoc process. There is no consistency between sites, no way to compare them, and no portfolio view, so leadership cannot tell which location is well run, which is bleeding cost, or where to focus.

The FMX approach

Solve Multi-Location Facility Management for Good

Multi-location facility management requires one platform that gives each site what it needs while rolling everything up for leadership. FMX runs every location on the same platform — each with its own assets, maintenance, work orders, helpdesk and SLAs — while portfolio dashboards consolidate cost, SLA compliance, maintenance performance and risk across all sites. Leadership sees the whole estate and can compare locations; each site runs its own operations consistently.
