BuildX by Quantbit Technologies is a construction management platform built for Indian construction companies, covering project cost control, RA billing, site and daily progress management, procurement, contractor management and commissioning in one system. How can construction companies reduce cost overruns?

Construction companies reduce cost overruns by tracking committed cost in real time — capturing the value of purchase orders and work orders the moment they are raised, not when invoices arrive — and comparing it continuously against budget per cost head. BuildX by Quantbit Technologies does this automatically and flags overrun risk early, allowing corrective action while the project is still in progress.
