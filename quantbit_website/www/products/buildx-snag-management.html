Snag Management, Built Into the BuildX Platform

At the end of a project, the snag list decides whether the client is delighted or frustrated — yet snags are often captured on paper, photographed on personal phones, and chased over phone calls, so items get lost and handover drags on. The BuildX Snag Management module makes snags fast to capture and impossible to lose: capture with photos on mobile, assign to the responsible contractor, track to verified closure, and report exactly how many remain between the project and a clean handover.

What is snag management in construction?

Snag management in construction is the process of capturing, assigning, tracking and closing the defects and incomplete items (snags) identified before handover — ideally with photo evidence and verified closure. It directly determines client satisfaction at handover. The BuildX Snag Management module by Quantbit Technologies manages snags from mobile photo capture through verified closure to handover-readiness reporting.
