Built for your challenges

Three inventory challenges specific to Ayurvedic pharmacies

Standard pharmacy software was designed for allopathic medicines with fixed compositions and standard batch attributes. Ayurvedic inventory behaves differently — and needs a system that understands the difference.

Herb lot quality varies by season — standard batch tracking doesn't capture this

Ashwagandha root from the winter harvest is different from the summer harvest. A standard system that tracks only manufacturer batch and expiry misses the source and season information that matters for compound formulation quality.

Compound formulations prepared in-house — no system link between raw herb and finished compound

When a Kashaya or Churna is prepared from raw herbs in the hospital dispensary, there is no automatic link between the raw herb lots consumed and the finished compound batch. Traceability is lost at formulation.

Shelf-life varies by formulation type — churnas, ghirtas, and aristas expire differently

A Ghrita (medicated ghee) has a different shelf-life than a Churna (powder) or an Arista (fermented liquid). Systems that apply the same shelf-life logic to all items miss the category-specific variation that drives expiry management in an Ayurvedic pharmacy.
