The problem

At the end of a project, the snag list decides whether the client is delighted or frustrated. Yet snags are often captured on paper walkthroughs, photographed on personal phones, and chased over phone calls — so items get lost, the same snag is raised twice, and handover drags on while everyone argues about what is actually still open.

The BuildX approach

Solve Snag List Management for Good

Snag management should be fast to capture and impossible to lose. BuildX lets site teams capture snags with photos on mobile during the walkthrough, assigns each to the responsible contractor or team, tracks every snag to verified closure, and reports exactly how many remain between the project and handover. The snag list stops being a source of disputes and becomes a clear, shared path to a clean handover.

What is snag list management in construction?

Snag list management in construction is the process of capturing, assigning, tracking and closing the defects and incomplete items (snags) identified before handover — ideally with photo evidence and verified closure.
