What is RA billing in construction?

RA (Running Account) billing in construction is progressive billing against measured quantities of work completed, done cycle by cycle, with retention held against milestones, mobilisation and material advances recovered, and statutory deductions (such as TDS and GST) applied each time. It applies to both client billing and subcontractor billing.
