BuildX for EPC

EPC contracts are large, long and complex — engineering, procurement and construction must stay synchronised across multiple packages, dozens of subcontractors and a procurement spend that can dominate the contract value. A single mismanaged procurement package or unreconciled subcontractor account can swing an EPC project from profit to loss. BuildX gives EPC contractors integrated control over the full lifecycle, from engineering hand-off and procurement through subcontracting, cost control and RA billing to commissioning.
