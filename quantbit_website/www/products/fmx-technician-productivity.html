The problem

A technician's day is full of waste that has nothing to do with their skill: driving back to the office for the next job, filling out paper forms, arriving at an asset without its history, returning a second time because they did not have the right part. Every one of these steals time from actual work — and frustrates good technicians.

The FMX approach

Solve Improve Technician Productivity for Good

Improving technician productivity means removing the friction around the work, not pushing people harder. FMX puts work orders on the technician's mobile app so they get the next job without driving back; allocates jobs by skill, location and load so the right person goes to the right job; gives asset history and checklists in hand so they arrive prepared; and captures completion on site so paperwork disappears. Technicians spend their time fixing things, and first-time-fix rates rise.

How can facilities improve technician productivity?

Facilities improve technician productivity by removing the friction around the work rather than pushing people harder — putting work orders on a mobile app so technicians stop driving back for instructions, allocating jobs by skill and location, giving asset history and checklists in hand so technicians arrive prepared and fix issues first time, and capturing completion on site to eliminate paperwork. FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides all of these and tracks jobs per technician and first-time-fix rate.

What reduces facility technician efficiency?

Facility technician efficiency is reduced by non-work activities: travelling back to the office for the next job, filling out paper forms, arriving at an asset without its history or the right part, and making repeat visits to finish a job. A mobile-first system that delivers jobs, asset history and checklists in the field, and captures completion on site, removes this waste. FMX by Quantbit Technologies is built to do exactly that.
