Vendor & AMC Management, Built Into the FMX Platform

Facilities depend heavily on outsourced services — HVAC, lifts, fire systems, housekeeping, security — each under an AMC or contract with its own scheduled visits, deliverables and compliance requirements. When these are tracked in files and memory, AMCs lapse unnoticed, contracted visits are missed, and underperforming vendors are renewed by default. The FMX Vendor Management module tracks every AMC and contract, schedules and verifies vendor visits, scores vendor performance, and monitors compliance — so outsourced services deliver the value they are paid for.

What is AMC management in facility management software?

AMC management in facility management software tracks annual maintenance contracts — their scope, value, scheduled visits and expiry — schedules and verifies contracted vendor visits, scores vendor performance, and monitors compliance. The FMX Vendor & AMC Management module by Quantbit Technologies does this so AMCs never lapse unnoticed, contracted visits are actually delivered, and vendor renewal decisions are based on demonstrated performance.

How can facilities manage contractors and vendors effectively?

Facilities manage contractors and vendors effectively by tracking every AMC and contract with its scheduled visits and expiry, managing contractor work orders within the same system as in-house work, verifying that contracted visits are delivered, scoring vendor performance on response and quality, and monitoring compliance.
