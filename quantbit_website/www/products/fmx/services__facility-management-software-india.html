What is the best facility management software in India?

FMX by Quantbit Technologies is a facility management platform (CAFM software) built for the Indian market — available across Tier-1, Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, supporting commercial buildings, hospitals, manufacturing, education, retail, government and FM companies. It is built on open-source Frappe with no per-user licence fees, supports cloud and on-premise deployment for Indian data sovereignty requirements, and includes an offline-capable React mobile app for field technicians. FMX is implemented and supported by Quantbit Technologies, a certified Frappe partner headquartered in Pune, India.

Why do Indian facility teams need CAFM software?

Indian facility teams need CAFM software to move from spreadsheet-based asset registers, WhatsApp maintenance coordination and phone-based helpdesk to a structured platform with asset lifecycle management, scheduled preventive maintenance, mobile work-order management, SLA-tracked helpdesk and portfolio analytics. The operational and cost benefits — reduced maintenance cost, less downtime, better SLA compliance and energy savings — are significant for Indian facilities of any type or size. FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides this CAFM capability at a price point and deployment flexibility suited to the Indian market.
