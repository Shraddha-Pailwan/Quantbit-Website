FMX Serving Surat Facility Teams

Surat is one of India's fastest-growing cities and a global centre for diamond processing and textile manufacturing — handling over 90% of the world's rough diamond cutting and polishing, and housing one of India's largest textile industries in its textile markets and processing clusters. The Surat Diamond Bourse, one of the world's largest commercial buildings, represents the ambition of Surat's business community. The Sachin and Hazira industrial estates house chemical, engineering and petro-chemical manufacturing, while Hazira's industrial zone includes major chemical and LNG facilities. Commercial real estate in the new Surat areas — Vesu, Pal, Dumas Road — has grown rapidly, a new private hospital sector has emerged, and Surat's Smart City status drives technology adoption.
