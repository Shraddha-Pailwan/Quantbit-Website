FMX for Government Facilities Facilities

Government facility management operates under constraints that private sector organisations do not face: public accountability, procurement transparency, statutory compliance, audit requirements and limited budgets that must be visibly and justifiably spent. A government facility team that cannot show what assets it manages, what maintenance was done, which contractors were paid and whether value was delivered is a compliance and governance risk. FMX brings accountability to government facility management — a documented asset register, transparent procurement, work-order audit trails, contractor accountability and multi-department helpdesk — on open-source ERPNext infrastructure that is free from proprietary lock-in.
