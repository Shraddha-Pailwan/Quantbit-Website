Quantbit adds MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet) attachments to item masters, configures hazardous material classifications, and builds storage condition controls in ERPNext. Regulatory reporting fields for REACH compliance, environmental reporting, and hazardous waste management are added as custom modules. These controls ensure compliance documentation is always linked to inventory transactions.
Yes. Quantbit configures milestone-based billing in ERPNext where invoices are generated automatically when a project milestone is marked complete and approved. For BOQ-based billing, the completed quantity percentage is linked to the customer invoice calculation. Both models are common in Vadodara EPC companies and Quantbit has implemented both variations.
ERPNext Project module is not a replacement for tools like MS Project or Primavera for complex scheduling. ERPNext excels at the financial and procurement side of project management — costs, budgets, procurement, subcontractor billing, and invoicing. Quantbit often integrates ERPNext with MS Project or a simpler scheduling tool so engineers manage the Gantt chart while ERPNext manages the money.
