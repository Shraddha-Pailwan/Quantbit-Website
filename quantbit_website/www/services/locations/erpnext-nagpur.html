ERPNext in Nagpur — Answered

WMSPro is Quantbit's ERPNext product for warehouse and 3PL management. For Nagpur 3PL operators, it provides multi-client inventory management (each client's goods tracked separately), location-level stock (rack, bay, bin), inbound and outbound workflows with barcode scanning, and automated client billing based on transactions, storage space, or value. Client portals give customers real-time visibility into their inventory.
FleetX is Quantbit's ERPNext product for fleet management. It covers vehicle master management, trip planning and tracking, driver management, fuel consumption logging, maintenance scheduling, tyre management, and trip-wise revenue and cost analysis. For Nagpur transport companies, FleetX provides the operational visibility to manage profitability by vehicle, route, and driver — replacing the registers and spreadsheets most use today.
Quantbit configures ERPNext for agro commodity procurement with seasonal procurement planning, mandi purchase management, quality sampling on arrival, weighbridge integration, and farmer payment management. Warehouse receipts (when storing farmer produce) and processing plant operations are tracked. For orange and cotton procurement in Vidarbha, Quantbit has built specific workflows for lot-wise grading and packing.
