Industry-specific questions

ERPNext in Hyderabad — Answered

ERPNext's Manufacturing module can be configured for GMP compliance with batch manufacturing records (BMR), standard operating procedures linked to work orders, in-process quality checks, and deviation management. Quantbit configures ERPNext with pharma-specific workflows including batch review and approval, yield calculation, and packaging reconciliation. While ERPNext is not an out-of-the-box FDA 21 CFR Part 11 compliant system, Quantbit can implement the audit trail and electronic signature controls required for regulated pharma companies.
ERPNext's batch traceability covers the complete batch lifecycle — from raw material receipt (with supplier batch number) through in-process production to finished goods and customer delivery. In a recall scenario, ERPNext can identify every customer who received a specific batch and generate a recall list instantly. Quantbit configures batch-wise expiry management, FIFO/FEFO stock, and recall workflow automation.
Quantbit builds pharma-specific custom fields and workflows in ERPNext for drug licence management — tracking licence numbers, validity dates, product-wise authorisations, and renewal alerts. Regulatory submission documents (dossiers, stability data) are stored as attachments linked to item masters. Licence-wise inventory restrictions prevent sale of products under expired licences.
Yes. ERPNext's Project module supports time-and-material billing, milestone billing, and retainer billing for IT services companies. Timesheets captured by employees link to projects and generate invoices. Resource utilisation reports track billable vs bench time. Quantbit has implemented ERPNext for Hyderabad IT companies replacing multiple tools (Jira billing, Zoho HR, QuickBooks) with a single integrated platform.
For basic lab inventory and sample tracking, ERPNext's Quality module and custom extensions built by Quantbit can replace simple LIMS implementations. For regulated labs with complex instrument integration, method management, and certificate of analysis generation, Quantbit evaluates whether ERPNext customisation is the right approach or whether an integration between ERPNext (for procurement, HR, finance) and a dedicated LIMS is more appropriate.
For pharma companies in Hyderabad, the most critical ERPNext modules are: Quality Management (QC, batch testing, release), Manufacturing (BMR, production orders, yield), Inventory (batch tracking, expiry, FIFO), Accounts (GST, e-invoicing, cost centre accounting), and HR & Payroll. Many pharma companies also implement the Asset module for equipment qualification tracking and preventive maintenance.
