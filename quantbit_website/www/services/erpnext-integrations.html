The Process

Three Steps to a Live Integration

We have done this integration dozens of times across manufacturing, retail, healthcare, and facility management companies. Here is what the journey looks like.

1

Discovery & Mapping

We spend one or two sessions understanding which tools you use today, what data needs to flow where, and what your team should never have to do manually again. We map every field and every trigger before writing a single line of configuration.

2

Configure & Test

3

Go Live & Support

Once live, the integration runs in the background — fetching, pushing, and reconciling data automatically. Our team monitors it for the first 30 days and remains available for any adjustments as your business processes evolve.

FAQs

Questions We Hear Most Often

Does ERPNext support Razorpay integration natively?
ERPNext has a basic Razorpay payment link feature in newer versions, but it does not handle automated reconciliation, payment status webhooks, or multi-currency settlement out of the box. Quantbit's Core Business Connector extends this with full reconciliation automation, payment link generation from sales invoices, and real-time bank matching — which is where most finance teams actually need the help.
Can ERPNext send WhatsApp messages to customers automatically?
Yes — with the Communication Connector, ERPNext can trigger WhatsApp messages automatically when a sales order is confirmed, an invoice is raised, a delivery is dispatched, or a payment reminder is due. Messages are sent through the official WhatsApp Business API, which means they are delivered reliably, support rich media like PDFs, and comply with WhatsApp's commerce policies.
How does ERPNext integrate with biometric attendance devices?
Biometric devices from ZKTeco, ESSL, and similar manufacturers export attendance logs via a local API or FTP push. Quantbit's HR integration fetches these logs on a scheduled basis, matches them against the ERPNext employee master, and creates attendance records automatically — including late-entry flags, early-exit records, and overtime calculations based on your shift policy.
Will integrations survive ERPNext version upgrades?
Quantbit's connector packs are built using ERPNext's official hooks and custom app architecture — not monkey patches or core file edits. This means they survive version upgrades cleanly in the vast majority of cases. When a major ERPNext release does change an underlying API, we update the connector pack and push the update to all affected clients as part of the subscription.
Is ERPNext integration available for businesses in Oman and the GCC?
Yes. Quantbit has a physical office in Muscat and has implemented ERPNext integrations for businesses in Oman, UAE, and across the GCC. The payment connector supports local bank APIs, Oman VAT-compliant invoice formats, and Arabic WhatsApp notifications. The Industrial IoT pack has been deployed in Omani manufacturing facilities. All support is available in Gulf Standard Time.
