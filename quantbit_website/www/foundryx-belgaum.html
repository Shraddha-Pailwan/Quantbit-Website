Belgaum's Automotive Facts

The Belgaum Foundry Cluster — Karnataka's Automotive Casting Powerhouse

Belgaum's foundry industry came up primarily to cater to the automotive sector at Pune and has grown into an internationally recognised cluster. With approximately 100 foundry units spread across Udyambag and Macche industrial areas, Belgaum is noted for high-precision castings, a significant ISO certification rate (~20% of units hold ISO 9000), and a meaningful export orientation. The proximity to Goa's port infrastructure also makes Belgaum an efficient export hub.

Pain Points We Solve

ERP Challenges Specific to Belgaum's Export-Oriented Foundries

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

📄

Export documentation prepared manually

Material Test Reports and Certificates of Conformance for European customers prepared in Excel and Word, prone to errors and taking 2–3 days per shipment.

🔍

Customer complaints without traceability

Export customers raise defect claims months after shipment. Without digital heat records, Belgaum foundries cannot defend or identify root cause.

❌

Rejection rate variance across shifts

Night shift rejection rates consistently higher than day shift, but without heat-wise, shift-wise rejection analysis, the root cause is invisible.

🏷️

Pattern management for 200+ tooling sets

Large Belgaum foundries maintain extensive pattern libraries. Physical tracking in registers leads to production stoppages when patterns cannot be located.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions foundry owners ask us before implementing FoundryX.

Does FoundryX generate export documentation for Belgaum foundries?
Yes. FoundryX auto-generates all documents required for export shipments: Material Test Reports (MTR) with full chemical composition per heat, Certificates of Conformance (CoC), heat-specific packing lists, and dimensional inspection reports. These are generated in English in the formats expected by European and American automotive customers.
Can FoundryX support both Udyambag and Macche plant operations together?
Yes. FoundryX supports multi-plant configuration within a single ERPNext instance. Foundries with operations in both Udyambag and Macche industrial areas can manage both plants from a single dashboard with consolidated reporting and plant-wise drill-down.
How does FoundryX handle ISO 9001 documentation requirements for Belgaum foundries?
FoundryX maintains inspection records, calibration schedules, non-conformance reports, corrective action registers, and process control documentation — all linked to production records. This creates the digital audit trail required for ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 certification renewals without manual record keeping.
What is the approximate cost of FoundryX for a Belgaum foundry with 30 users?
FoundryX is built on open-source ERPNext — so there are no per-user licence fees. You pay for implementation, configuration, and annual support. For a 30-user Belgaum foundry, total cost of ownership is typically 40–60% lower than proprietary foundry ERP alternatives over 3 years.
Does FoundryX work for both grey iron and alloy steel castings made in Belgaum?
Yes. FoundryX is metal-agnostic. It handles grey iron, SG (ductile) iron, alloy steel, and aluminium casting workflows. For alloy steel, it tracks alloy addition records, heat treatment parameters, and hardness test results — all linked to the heat number.
