FAQ

Questions From Sugar Factory Owners in This Belt

What factory GMs, cooperative secretaries, and IT heads ask us before implementing SugarX.

Does SugarX have local implementation support in Kolhapur?
Yes. Quantbit Technologies has a dedicated office at Unchgaon Industrial Area, Kolhapur. Our Kolhapur team handles all implementation, training, and ongoing support for sugar factory clients in the Kolhapur, Sangli, and Satara belt — providing on-site support during the crushing season that Pune-based or Mumbai-based ERP vendors cannot match.
Is SugarX configured for Maharashtra SSK (cooperative sugar factory) workflows?
Yes. SugarX is built with cooperative factory workflows natively: SSK farmer membership structure, cane supply agreements by plot and variety, FRP payment register in Maharashtra Sugar Commissioner format, gang-wise seasonal worker attendance, and the specific reports required for cooperative management committee review. These are not add-ons — they are core features built from real cooperative implementations in the Kolhapur belt.
How does SugarX handle FRP payment compliance for the Maharashtra Sugar Commissioner?
SugarX calculates FRP per farmer automatically from actual cane weight delivered and the Maharashtra state advisory price. It generates the payment schedule, NEFT bank transfer file for direct farmer payment, SMS notification to farmers on payment credit, and the payment register in the exact format required by the Maharashtra Sugar Commissioner. No manual reformatting is needed before statutory submissions.
Can SugarX integrate with the weighbridge controllers used in Kolhapur factories?
Yes. SugarX integrates with the weighbridge controller models commonly deployed across Kolhapur sugar factories. Weight data is captured automatically — gross weight, tare weight, and net cane weight — and the T-Cane billing slip is generated within 30 seconds of vehicle exit. Our Kolhapur team visits your weighbridge setup during the pre-implementation phase to confirm integration compatibility before go-live.
How long does SugarX implementation take for a Kolhapur cooperative sugar factory?
A standard SugarX implementation for a Kolhapur cooperative factory covering T-Cane billing, FRP payments, cane procurement, crushing production, and finance takes 14–20 weeks in phases. Phase 1 — T-Cane billing and FRP payment automation go-live before the crushing season — takes 6–8 weeks. Our Kolhapur team works on-site throughout the season to ensure the system handles the full crushing load from day one.
