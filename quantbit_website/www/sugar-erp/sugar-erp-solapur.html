Pain Points We Solve

ERP Challenges Specific to Solapur's Large Cooperative Sugar Factories

The challenges that bring factory GMs and cooperative management to SugarX — and how we solve them.

⚖️

T-Cane Billing Can't Keep Up with Volume

At 1,000+ vehicle entries per day across peak season, manual billing falls behind by 3–5 days. Farmers waiting for payment calculation. Disputes from weight register errors. Night shift teams entering previous day's data manually.

🌾

Crop Estimation Without GPS Data

Procurement planning based on farmer self-declared cane area leads to crushing shortfalls or excess cane at yard. No GPS-verified variety data means incorrect recovery estimates for production planning.

📋

10,000-Farmer FRP Register Preparation

Manual compilation of FRP payment register for 10,000+ farmers takes 4–5 working days per payment cycle. Excel calculations risk errors. Sugar Commissioner submission always rushed at the deadline.

🏭

Distillery and Crushing on Separate Systems

Solapur factories with distillery annexes track molasses on paper or in Excel separately from the main factory system. No live view of molasses stock, fermentation status, or ethanol dispatch from the same dashboard.

FAQ

Questions From Sugar Factory Owners in This Belt

What factory GMs, cooperative secretaries, and IT heads ask us before implementing SugarX.

Can SugarX handle the scale of Solapur's large cooperative factories with 15,000+ farmers?
Yes. SugarX is built for large-scale cooperative sugar factory operations. The T-Cane billing module handles 1,000+ vehicle entries per day across multiple weighbridge lanes without performance degradation. The FRP payment module calculates, schedules, and generates NEFT bank files for 15,000+ farmer members automatically — what takes a manual accounts team 4–5 days is completed in minutes.
How does SugarX help with cane procurement planning for Solapur's drought-prone geography?
SugarX includes a GPS cane field survey mobile app that field supervisors use to record plot-wise variety, actual cane area, and estimated tonnage based on crop condition. This GPS-verified data replaces farmer self-declarations for procurement planning — significantly improving crushing plan accuracy and reducing the gap between projected and actual cane arrival.
Does SugarX cover distillery operations for Solapur factories expanding into ethanol?
Yes. SugarX covers the complete integrated sugar complex including distillery operations: molasses receipt from crushing, fermentation batch tracking with IoT sensor integration, ENA and rectified spirit production, ethanol dispatch with excise gate pass and DGTD compliance reports, and ethanol blending programme (EBP) supply records. All integrated with the main crushing and finance modules.
How does SugarX generate the FRP payment register for the Maharashtra Sugar Commissioner?
SugarX maintains farmer-wise cane receipt records throughout the season and calculates FRP per farmer based on actual weight, the Maharashtra state advisory price, and recovery percentage. At each payment cycle, it generates the payment register in the exact format required by the Maharashtra Sugar Commissioner, plus the NEFT/RTGS bank transfer file for direct payment to farmer bank accounts.
What is the typical implementation timeline for a large Solapur sugar factory?
For a large Solapur cooperative factory with full scope — T-Cane billing, FRP payments, cane procurement, crushing production, distillery, HRMS, and finance — the typical timeline is 16–22 weeks in four phases. The highest priority is getting T-Cane billing and FRP payment modules live before the crushing season starts, which our team targets in the first 6–8 weeks.
