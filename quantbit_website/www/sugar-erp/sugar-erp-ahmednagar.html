Pain Points We Solve

ERP Challenges Common to Ahmednagar–Nashik Cooperative Sugar Factories

The challenges that bring factory GMs and cooperative management to SugarX — and how we solve them.

🌾

Variety Mix Not Tracked Through Processing

High recovery belt depends on variety-specific processing parameters. But most factories track variety only at the farm — by the time cane reaches the weighbridge, variety data is lost. SugarX links variety records from GPS survey through billing to lab recovery.

📊

Recovery Reporting Always 24 Hours Behind

Daily recovery — the most watched number in Ahmednagar factories — compiled manually from shift records and lab data. Factory GM gets yesterday's number at 10am today. SugarX makes recovery visible on a live dashboard in real time.

📋

FRP Register Manual Compilation Before Submission

Accounts team spending 3–4 days manually compiling and formatting FRP payment register before each Sugar Commissioner submission deadline. Errors in farmer account numbers or payment amounts create disputes and reprocessing.

🏭

No Integration Between Sugar and Distillery Operations

Factories with attached distilleries manage sugar and distillery on separate systems. Molasses transfer tracked on paper. No live view of ethanol stock, fermentation batch status, or excise compliance record from factory management dashboard.

FAQ

Questions From Sugar Factory Owners in This Belt

What factory GMs, cooperative secretaries, and IT heads ask us before implementing SugarX.

Does SugarX support variety-wise cane tracking for Ahmednagar's high-recovery varieties?
Yes. SugarX GPS cane field survey app records variety-wise crop area and tonnage estimates per plot during field surveys. This variety data is linked to the farmer's cane agreement and flows through to T-Cane billing and lab analysis records. The factory gets variety-wise arrival analysis, variety-wise recovery comparison, and variety-mix planning support — all from live data rather than estimates.
How does SugarX help Ahmednagar factories optimise recovery rates?
SugarX lab module records juice quality parameters — pol%, fibre%, purity, Brix — per shift and links them to the variety and plot data from procurement. The daily recovery dashboard shows real-time recovery versus target, shift-wise trends, and variety-wise comparison. This gives process managers the information to adjust crushing parameters during the season, not in retrospect.
Is SugarX suitable for the Pravara Cooperative Sugar Factory region's large farmer membership?
Yes. SugarX handles large cooperative farmer memberships — full farmer master with Aadhaar linking, plot GPS records, cane variety registration, supply agreements, and payment history. FRP payment calculation and register generation are automated regardless of farmer count. The system has been implemented in cooperative factories in Maharashtra with farmer memberships exceeding 10,000.
Does SugarX have a multilingual interface for Ahmednagar's Marathi-speaking workforce?
Yes. SugarX runs on ERPNext which supports Marathi language interface. Shop-floor operators, cane department supervisors, and accounts staff can use the system in Marathi, while the management and export reporting functions are available in English. This is particularly valuable for Ahmednagar's cooperative workforce where senior operators may be more comfortable in Marathi.
What is the implementation approach for a cooperative factory in the Ahmednagar belt?
Quantbit follows a phased implementation: Phase 1 deploys T-Cane billing and FRP payment automation before the crushing season — this is the highest priority and takes 6–8 weeks. Phases 2 and 3 cover cane procurement, GPS field app, crushing production, and distillery. Phase 4 covers finance integration and the management dashboard. The complete implementation for an Ahmednagar cooperative typically takes 14–20 weeks.
