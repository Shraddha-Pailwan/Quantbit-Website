The Challenge

Four Critical Operational Problems Before SugarX ERP

At the scale of a ₹1200 Crore sugar mill handling thousands of farmers, daily crushing operations, and a distillery complex, manual processes were creating compounding problems every season. Here is what Venkateshwara Power Project was dealing with before implementation.

⚖️

Manual T-Cane Billing Taking 5–7 Days

Cane weighment data from the weighbridge was recorded in paper registers and manually transferred to billing ledgers. A single season's T-Cane billing involved thousands of vehicle entries across multiple shifts — processed by a team of clerks over days, with disputes arising from weight discrepancies and calculation errors.

Impact: Farmers waiting days for cane receipts. Disputes at the gate every morning. Finance team reconciling billing for weeks after crushing ended.
💳

Farmer FRP Payment Delays of 30–60 Days

Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) payments to farmers were calculated manually from billing registers, compiled into payment lists in Excel, and submitted to the bank in batches. The process from cane delivery to payment took 30–60 days — causing financial stress for farming families and tension with the cooperative leadership.

Impact: 30–60 day payment lag. Farmer complaints to State Sugar Commissioner. Risk of statutory penalty for delayed FRP payment.
🔗

Crushing, Distillery & Finance on Separate Systems

The crushing plant, distillery, and finance department operated on different software tools and manual registers with no data exchange between them. Recovery percentages were calculated at month-end from consolidated data — meaning the management team could not see real-time sugar recovery, molasses generation, or ethanol output during the crushing season.

Impact: Management flying blind during crushing. Month-end closing taking 10+ days of manual reconciliation across three departments.
👷

1,000+ Workers on Manual HRMS & Payroll

Managing seasonal workers, permanent staff, gang-wise attendance, and the PF/ESI compliance for 1,000+ workers was done through multiple registers and spreadsheets. Payroll preparation for the crushing season took weeks. Statutory returns for PF, ESI, and professional tax were consistently filed late.

Impact: Payroll delays, compliance penalties, inability to scale workforce tracking during peak crushing season.
The Solution

SugarX ERP Modules Deployed at Venkateshwara Power Project

Quantbit Technologies implemented a phased rollout of SugarX ERP — built on the open-source ERPNext platform — covering cane procurement through finance. All modules share a single database, eliminating the data silos that previously slowed operations.

01

Farmer Registration & Cane Agreement Management

Digital farmer database with Aadhaar linking, plot GPS mapping, variety-wise cane registration, and season-wise cane supply agreements. Farmer data migrated from legacy registers during pre-implementation phase.

02

Weighbridge Integration & T-Cane Billing

Direct integration with the factory weighbridge controller. Gross and tare weights captured automatically, T-Cane bill generated in under 30 seconds per vehicle, and data pushed to farmer payment module instantly.

03

FRP Farmer Payment Automation

Automated FRP calculation based on delivery weight, recovery percentage, and state advisory price. NEFT/RTGS bank file generation for direct farmer payment. SMS notification to farmer on payment credit. Payment register for State Sugar Commissioner compliance.

04

Harvesting & Transport Trip Management

Digital trip sheets replacing paper challans. Harvester and transporter-wise trip tracking with GPS. Cane yard stock real-time visibility. Vehicle turnaround time monitoring to optimise factory yard congestion.

05

Crushing & Factory Process ERP

Real-time tracking of juice extraction, clarification, evaporation, crystallisation, and centrifugation. Daily recovery percentage dashboard. Sugar production by grade (S-30, M-30, L-30). Molasses generation tracking.

06

Distillery & Ethanol Operations

Molasses receipt from crushing, fermentation tracking, distillation records, and ethanol dispatch with excise bond register. DGTD compliance reports. ENA, RS, and ethanol grade management. IoT sensor data integration for real-time fermentation monitoring.

07

Inventory, Stores & Sugar Dispatch

Godown-wise sugar stock by grade and packing. Sales order to dispatch workflow. e-Way bill generation integrated. Customer-wise delivery and billing. By-product dispatch (molasses, press mud, bagasse) tracking.

08

HRMS & Payroll for 1,000+ Workers

Biometric attendance integration, gang-wise attendance sheets, shift scheduling for 3-shift crushing operations. Automated payroll with PF, ESI, PT compliance. Employee self-service app. Seasonal vs permanent staff separation.

09

Finance, GST & Compliance Reporting

Integrated accounting with auto-posting from all operational modules. GST returns (GSTR-1, 3B), TDS, and e-way bill generation. Sugar Commissioner statutory reports auto-generated from live data. Month-end closing in hours instead of days.

10

Management Dashboard & Analytics

Real-time crushing season dashboard: daily cane crushed, sugar recovery %, farmer payments status, distillery throughput, and working capital position — all visible in one screen for the MD and CFO.

FAQ

Sugar ERP — Common Questions from Mill Owners

Questions sugar mill owners and IT heads ask before implementing SugarX ERP.

What is T-Cane billing in a sugar mill and how does ERP automate it?
T-Cane billing is the process of weighing incoming sugarcane from farmers at the factory weighbridge, recording gross and tare weight, calculating net cane weight, and generating a billing slip for each farmer or transporter. In manual systems this involves paper challan books, register entries, and manual calculations — causing delays, errors, and farmer disputes. SugarX ERP integrates directly with the weighbridge controller, captures weights automatically, generates the T-Cane bill in seconds, and feeds the data into the farmer payment and finance modules simultaneously.
How does SugarX ERP automate farmer FRP payments?
SugarX ERP calculates each farmer's Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) payment based on the actual cane weight delivered, the applicable state advisory price, and the recovery percentage of the season. It generates payment schedules, calculates installment amounts, produces bank transfer files (NEFT/RTGS) for direct credit to farmer accounts, and sends automated SMS or WhatsApp payment notifications. The system also maintains the payment register required by the State Sugar Commissioner for statutory compliance.
Can SugarX ERP manage both crushing operations and distillery in the same system?
Yes. SugarX ERP covers the complete value chain from sugarcane procurement through crushing (juice extraction, clarification, evaporation, crystallisation) to packing and dispatch, as well as distillery operations including molasses receipt, fermentation, distillation, and ethanol dispatch with excise compliance. All modules share a single ERPNext database, so cane recovery, sugar production, molasses generation, and ethanol output are visible on a unified dashboard in real time.
What is the typical implementation timeline for SugarX ERP at a large Maharashtra sugar mill?
For a large Maharashtra cooperative or private sugar mill managing 5,000–15,000 farmers and 1,500–3,000 tonnes per day crushing capacity, a full SugarX ERP implementation typically takes 16–24 weeks covering all modules: farmer management, T-Cane billing, production, inventory, distillery, HRMS, and finance. Quantbit follows a phased approach, going live with cane billing and farmer payments first — the highest-priority modules — before subsequent phases covering production and finance.
How does SugarX ERP ensure statutory compliance for Maharashtra sugar mills?
SugarX ERP automates all statutory compliance requirements for Maharashtra sugar mills: FRP payment registers for the State Sugar Commissioner, GST returns, e-way bill generation for sugar and ethanol dispatch, DGTD compliance reports for distillery, PF and ESI returns for factory workers, and the monthly production reports required by the Sugar Directorate. Reports are generated directly from live operational data — eliminating the manual compilation that typically takes accounting teams 2–3 days per month.
