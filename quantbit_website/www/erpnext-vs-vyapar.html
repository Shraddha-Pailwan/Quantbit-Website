7 Signals You've Outgrown Vyapar

If you're seeing these in your business, it's time to assess ERPNext.

Signs your business has outgrown Vyapar:
  • You have 5+ employees and manage payroll in a spreadsheet or separate software
  • You run a workshop, factory, or production unit and track jobs/orders on paper or Excel
  • You have 2+ godowns or branches and struggle to get a single stock view
  • Purchase orders need approval from a manager before vendor payments
  • You have a sales team and track leads, follow-ups, and quotations manually
  • Your CA or accountant is asking for more structured books than Vyapar provides
  • You want your team to work from anywhere — field, warehouse, office — in real time
I run a Kolhapur engineering workshop with 12 employees and use Vyapar for billing. When should I switch to ERPNext?
The signal for a job-shop workshop is when you start losing track of which job is at which stage, what material was consumed per job, and what each job actually cost vs quoted. Vyapar can't give you job card tracking, material issue to production, labour cost per job, or rejection analysis. ERPNext's manufacturing module handles all of this — and adds HR/payroll so you stop managing 12 employee salaries, PF, and ESI in Excel. Quantbit's implementation for a 12-employee engineering workshop typically runs ₹8L–₹14L one-time and ₹2L–₹3.5L/year, going live in 8–12 weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I switch from Vyapar to ERPNext? +
Consider switching when: your business has 5+ employees and needs HR/payroll, you're running a manufacturing or workshop operation needing production tracking, you have multiple godowns or warehouses, you need purchase order approval workflows, or your monthly turnover crosses ₹25–50L and you need tighter financial controls. Vyapar is excellent for micro-businesses. ERPNext is designed for businesses with operational complexity.
What does Vyapar not do that ERPNext does? +
Vyapar handles GST billing, basic inventory, and simple accounting well. ERPNext adds: full HR and payroll (PF, ESI, PT), manufacturing with BOM and work orders, multi-warehouse bin-level tracking, purchase order approval workflows, CRM and sales pipeline, quality control, project management, customer portal, REST API for integrations, and multi-company. Vyapar is a billing app; ERPNext is a business operating system.
What is the cost difference between Vyapar and ERPNext? +
Vyapar costs ₹3,499–₹7,999/year per device. For a 10-user team, approximately ₹15,000–₹30,000/year. ERPNext for the same team starts at ₹1.5L–₹3L/year on Frappe Cloud — higher, but with HR, payroll, manufacturing, multi-warehouse, CRM, and approvals included, replacing 5+ separate tools.
Can Vyapar data be migrated to ERPNext? +
Yes. Vyapar exports data in Excel/CSV format covering customers, suppliers, items, and transaction history. Quantbit imports Vyapar masters into ERPNext, maps GST tax types, sets up opening stock, and enters outstanding receivables/payables as opening balances. Migration typically takes 2–4 weeks.
Is ERPNext too complex for a small business? +
ERPNext can be configured for any complexity level. A 5-user trading company can use only Accounting, Purchase, Sales, and Inventory modules — as simple as Vyapar but with cloud access, multi-user roles, and better reporting. Quantbit configures ERPNext to match exactly what your business needs today, with modules activated as you grow.
