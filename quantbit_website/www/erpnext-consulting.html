What does an ERPNext consulting engagement include?

An ERPNext consulting engagement from Quantbit Technologies includes a fit assessment evaluating ERPNext against business processes, industry vertical and integration needs; a gap analysis mapping current processes to ERPNext workflows; a phased implementation roadmap with module sequence, timeline and resource requirements; an integration and technology audit; and a business case with ROI estimate. The engagement is time-boxed — typically 4–6 weeks — and delivers a clear go/no-go recommendation, not an open-ended advisory.

How do I know if ERPNext is right for my business?

ERPNext is well-suited to SMEs and mid-market businesses in manufacturing, trading, services, healthcare, education and project-based industries needing integrated accounting, inventory, HR, CRM and operations management. The most reliable way to confirm fit is a structured assessment with a certified implementation partner. Quantbit Technologies conducts ERPNext fit assessments evaluating process alignment, industry suitability, integration complexity and organisational readiness — and will recommend against ERPNext if it is not the right fit.
