The problem

On most construction projects, subcontractors represent the single largest cost — and the place where control is weakest. Loose rate agreements, work done beyond the order without re-pricing, over-billing that nobody catches, retention and advances that go untracked: each one leaks money, and together they can quietly consume a project's margin.

The BuildX approach

Solve Contractor Management for Good

Controlling subcontractor cost means controlling the whole relationship — from the work order and rate agreement, through measured-quantity bill certification, to retention, advance recovery and performance. BuildX puts the entire subcontractor flow in one controlled system, so every bill is checked against the order, every retention and advance is tracked, and contractor performance is visible across projects. The largest cost on your project becomes the most controlled.
