The problem

The daily progress report is the single source of truth for what happened on a construction site — yet it is often a paper diary or a WhatsApp message that never becomes usable data. Without structured, connected DPRs, head office manages on hearsay, disputes have no evidence, and the link between daily site activity and project status is broken.

The BuildX approach

Solve Daily Progress Reporting for Good

A good DPR system turns the daily site record into live management data. BuildX captures the DPR on mobile from the site — work done, manpower deployed, machinery active, material received and consumed, with photos and site instructions — even in low-connectivity conditions, and syncs it to head office. The result is a structured, searchable, photo-backed record that drives progress tracking, cost, billing and dispute resolution.

What is a daily progress report (DPR) in construction?

A daily progress report (DPR) in construction is the structured daily record of site activity — work executed, manpower deployed, machinery active, material received and consumed — usually with photos and site instructions. It is the primary source of truth for what happened on site each day.
