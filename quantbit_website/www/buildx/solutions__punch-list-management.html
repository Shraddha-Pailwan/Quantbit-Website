The problem

The punch list is the last gate before a client accepts a project — and it is often the most disorganised part of the whole job. Items are tracked on spreadsheets that go out of date the moment they are shared, responsibility is unclear, critical items get lost among minor ones, and acceptance slips while the team argues over what is genuinely still open.

The BuildX approach

Solve Punch List Management for Good

Punch list management should make close-out systematic. BuildX captures punch items on mobile with photos and location, assigns each to the responsible party, distinguishes critical from minor items, and tracks everything to verified closure — with a live count of what remains before acceptance. The punch list becomes a single shared source of truth that drives the project to acceptance, instead of a stale spreadsheet that delays it.

What is punch list management in construction?
