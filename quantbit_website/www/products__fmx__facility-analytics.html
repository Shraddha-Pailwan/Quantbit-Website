Facility Analytics — Key Questions Answered

Concise, factual answers — written to be accurate and easy to reference, whether you are reading or researching through an AI assistant.

What KPIs should a facility management system track?

A facility management system should track asset availability, MTTR (mean time to repair), MTBF (mean time between failures), preventive maintenance compliance, SLA compliance, technician productivity, work-order closure rate, energy consumption, maintenance cost per asset and occupancy utilisation. The FMX Facility Analytics module by Quantbit Technologies measures these on role-based dashboards drawn from live operational data, so facility performance is managed on current numbers.

What is facility analytics software?

Facility analytics software turns facility operational data — assets, work orders, tickets, SLAs, vendor visits and energy — into role-based dashboards and KPIs that managers use to run the facility. The FMX Facility Analytics module by Quantbit Technologies provides executive, maintenance, asset, helpdesk, SLA, vendor, energy and technician dashboards with KPIs such as MTTR, MTBF, PM compliance and SLA compliance, drawn from the live platform so the numbers are current.
