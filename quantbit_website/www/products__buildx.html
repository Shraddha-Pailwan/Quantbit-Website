A Construction Platform, Not a Generic ERP

Construction businesses do not lose money on the day a project ends — they lose it silently, week by week, through cost overruns nobody saw coming, contractor bills nobody reconciled, material that leaked off site, and progress that slipped without a paper trail. BuildX exists to make all of that visible in real time. What software is best for construction project cost control?

RA (Running Account) billing is the construction practice of billing progressively against measured work completed, with retention, recovery of advances and statutory deductions applied each cycle. BuildX handles RA billing end to end — measurement-based certification, deductions, retention release and contractor reconciliation — for both client billing and subcontractor bills.
