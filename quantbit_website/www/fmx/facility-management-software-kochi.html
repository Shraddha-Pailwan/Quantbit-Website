FMX Serving Kochi Facility Teams

Kochi is Kerala's commercial capital and one of South India's most dynamic cities — home to Infopark and SmartCity, two of the country's premier IT and technology parks housing global companies, a major international port and shipyard, Cochin International Airport, AIMS, Amrita and Aster hospitals, a large retail and hospitality sector anchored by Lulu Mall, and a growing commercial real estate market. The facility management demands in Kochi are shaped by its international orientation: IT campuses serving global clients demand international service standards; port and maritime facilities have critical uptime requirements; healthcare institutions serve a large medical tourism base with quality and compliance expectations to match; and the retail and hospitality sector demands the customer experience that facility reliability enables. FMX gives Kochi facility teams an internationally capable CAFM platform — asset lifecycle management, SLA-tracked helpdesk, mobile technician management, energy monitoring and portfolio analytics — delivered by a certified Frappe partner with a large in-house team.
