What is campus facility management software?

Universities improve campus facility management by unifying all buildings on one platform — with assets, maintenance and helpdesk per building and portfolio dashboards for the estate — running a tracked, SLA-monitored helpdesk for students and staff, managing space allocation and utilisation to use the campus efficiently, and monitoring energy across buildings toward sustainability targets.
