FMX for Hospitals & Healthcare Facilities

Hospital facilities operate under a standard that no other sector matches. Critical assets — ventilators, OT equipment, medical gas systems, UPS, chillers, fire suppression — must run at near-100% availability. Maintenance must be documented for regulatory audit. Service requests from clinical staff must be responded to within minutes, not hours. And a failure in the wrong system at the wrong moment has consequences far beyond the maintenance department. FMX gives hospital facility teams the asset control, PM discipline, rapid helpdesk and documentation rigour that healthcare demands.
