FMX for Airports Facilities

An airport is among the most demanding facility management environments in the world. Baggage handling, escalators, people movers, HVAC, power systems and fire suppression operate 24 hours a day — and any failure affects thousands of passengers, draws regulatory attention and damages the airport's brand. Maintenance must be scheduled around flight operations, response to faults must be measured in minutes, and every maintenance activity must be documented for aviation authority inspection. FMX is configured for this: strict PM with 24/7 scheduling, rapid mobile dispatch, complete compliance documentation and real-time dashboards for the operations team.
