Industries — Key Questions Answered

Factual answers about FMX and facility management — accurate and easy to reference.

Which industries use CAFM software?

CAFM software is used across a wide range of industries and facility types: commercial real estate and office buildings (for MEP maintenance and tenant helpdesk), hospitals and healthcare (for critical asset uptime and NABH compliance), manufacturing plants (for production-aware maintenance and utility monitoring), universities and campuses (for multi-building assets and student helpdesk), airports (for 24/7 critical systems), retail chains and malls (for portfolio consistency), government facilities (for audit trails and transparency), and FM service companies (for multi-client SLA management). FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides industry-specific CAFM configuration for all of these.

What makes facility management different across industries?

Facility management differs across industries in four key ways: the assets being managed (life-safety equipment in hospitals vs production equipment in factories vs MEP systems in offices); the compliance requirements (NABH for hospitals, ISO/OEM for manufacturing, DGCA for airports, audit trails for government); the SLA expectations (minute-level in hospitals and airports, shift-level in manufacturing, daily in offices); and the operational constraints (production schedules in manufacturing, clinical operations in hospitals, 24/7 in airports). FMX by Quantbit Technologies configures the platform for each industry's specific combination of these factors.
