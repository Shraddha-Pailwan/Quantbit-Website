FMX for Retail Chains & Malls Facilities

For a retail chain or shopping centre, facility management is an operational cost that directly touches the customer experience. A failed AC in a store on a summer afternoon, a broken escalator in a mall during peak hours, or a dark signage section — each one costs sales and erodes the brand. Managing 10, 50 or 100+ locations on separate spreadsheets means no consistency, no portfolio view and no way to ensure every store meets the same maintenance standard. FMX puts every store and mall on one platform — consistent maintenance, SLA-tracked helpdesk, HVAC and energy control, and portfolio dashboards that show which locations need attention.
