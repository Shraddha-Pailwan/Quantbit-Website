The problem

On site, the gap between a physical asset and its digital record is where facility management breaks down. A technician standing in front of a chiller cannot easily see its history, the occupant who spots a broken fixture cannot easily report it, and work done is recorded later, if at all. The information exists somewhere — just not where and when it is needed.

The FMX approach

Solve QR-Code Asset Management for Good

QR-code asset management closes the gap between the physical asset and its digital record. FMX lets you tag every asset with a QR code; scanning it on the FMX mobile app instantly opens that asset's full record — history, condition, warranty, open work orders. A technician can log work, an occupant can raise a ticket, and condition can be updated, all from the asset itself. And it works offline, so basements and plant rooms are no obstacle.

How does QR-code asset management work in facilities?

QR-code asset management tags each facility asset with a QR label linked to its digital record. Scanning the code on a mobile app instantly opens the asset's full history, condition, warranty and open work orders, and lets the scanner log work, update condition or raise a ticket against that exact asset on the spot. FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides this, works offline for low-signal locations, and improves data accuracy by logging work against the scanned asset.

What are the benefits of QR codes for facility asset tracking?

QR codes for facility asset tracking close the gap between the physical asset and its digital record: technicians scan to see history and log work at the asset, occupants scan to report issues on the exact asset, and work is recorded in the moment rather than later. This improves data accuracy (no wrong-asset entries) and works offline. FMX by Quantbit Technologies provides QR-code asset management within a full facility platform.
