Why FMX

One Platform for Complete Facility Management

Facility management is the discipline of keeping buildings, infrastructure and workplaces running — and it lives or dies on visibility. When assets are tracked in spreadsheets, maintenance is reactive, work orders are chased over phone calls and SLAs are measured after the fact, facilities cost more, fail more and frustrate the people who use them. FMX brings the entire operation onto one platform: a CAFM system that manages asset lifecycle, preventive and breakdown maintenance, a mobile technician workforce, helpdesk and SLAs, vendors, energy and space — so facility teams move from firefighting to control.

What is CAFM software and what does it do?

CAFM (Computer-Aided Facility Management) software helps organisations manage their facilities — assets, maintenance, work orders, helpdesk, SLAs, space, vendors and energy — from a single platform, typically with a mobile app for field technicians.
