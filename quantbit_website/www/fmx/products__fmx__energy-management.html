Energy Management, Built Into the FMX Platform

Energy is one of the largest costs in running a facility — and one of the least visible when it is only seen as a monthly utility bill. The FMX Energy Management module brings energy into the platform: it monitors utility consumption (integrating with smart meters and IoT where available), analyses consumption trends by facility and system, flags anomalies that signal waste or faults, and supports sustainability and carbon reporting. When energy use is visible at the level of buildings and systems, it becomes a cost that can be actively reduced.

Energy Management — Key Questions Answered

Concise, factual answers — written to be accurate and easy to reference, whether you are reading or researching through an AI assistant.

How does facility energy management software reduce costs?

Facility energy management software reduces costs by monitoring utility consumption at the level of buildings and systems (rather than as a single monthly bill), analysing trends, and flagging anomalies that signal waste, leaks or equipment faults so they can be fixed early. The FMX Energy Management module by Quantbit Technologies integrates smart meters and IoT for granular data, making energy a visible, actively reducible cost and supporting sustainability reporting.

What is energy monitoring in a CAFM platform?

Energy monitoring in a CAFM platform tracks a facility's utility consumption — electricity, water and other utilities — by facility, system and time, often integrating smart meters and IoT sensors, and analyses trends and anomalies to identify waste and efficiency opportunities. The FMX module by Quantbit Technologies provides this within the facility management platform and supports carbon and sustainability reporting from the same data.
