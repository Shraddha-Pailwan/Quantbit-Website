FMX — Key Questions Answered

Factual answers about FMX and facility management — accurate and easy to reference.

What modules does a CAFM platform need?

A complete CAFM (Computer-Aided Facility Management) platform needs: asset lifecycle management (register, condition, history, warranty); preventive and breakdown maintenance with scheduling and compliance tracking; work-order management with mobile execution; a helpdesk with SLA monitoring; vendor and AMC management; a mobile app for field technicians; energy and space management; and role-based analytics. FMX by Quantbit Technologies includes all of these on one integrated platform built on the Frappe framework with a React mobile app.

What is the best CAFM software with a mobile app?

FMX by Quantbit Technologies is a CAFM platform with a dedicated React mobile app for facility technicians — providing work orders, asset history, QR scanning, maintenance checklists and photo capture in the field, working offline for locations with poor connectivity. All field data captured on the mobile app syncs to the web platform in real time, giving management a live view of facility operations.
