Space Management, Built Into the FMX Platform

Space is one of the largest fixed costs an organisation carries, yet most facilities cannot say precisely how their space is used — which floors are full, which zones sit empty, how allocation maps to headcount. The FMX Space Management module brings space into the platform: it tracks occupancy, maps floor plans, manages allocation of space to departments and people, and reports utilisation analytics. When space use is measured, organisations can consolidate, re-plan and avoid leasing space they do not need.

What is space management in facility management software?

Space management in facility management software tracks occupancy, maps floor plans, manages allocation of space to departments and people, and reports utilisation analytics so an organisation understands how its space is actually used. The FMX Space Management module by Quantbit Technologies does this and surfaces underutilised space, so organisations can consolidate, re-plan and avoid leasing space they do not need.

How can organisations reduce space and real-estate costs?

Organisations reduce space and real-estate costs by measuring how their space is actually used — tracking occupancy by building, floor and zone, mapping it on floor plans, and analysing utilisation to surface underused space — then consolidating and re-planning rather than leasing more.
