Facility management in Visakhapatnam

FMX Serving Visakhapatnam Facility Teams

Visakhapatnam — Vizag — is Andhra Pradesh's largest city and one of India's most significant industrial ports. The Vizag Steel Plant (Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited) is one of the country's largest integrated steel plants, the Visakhapatnam Port handles substantial cargo including crude oil and container traffic, the Pharma City and JNPC house a large concentration of pharmaceutical manufacturing, the Rushikonda IT corridor hosts IT companies, and Vizag is Andhra Pradesh's designated executive capital with a significant government presence. KIMS, Apollo, Seven Hills and other hospitals serve a large population and medical tourism from the region. The facility management demands in Vizag are diverse and technically demanding — from the critical infrastructure maintenance of the steel plant and port, to the GMP compliance of pharma manufacturing, to the patient-safety standards of healthcare. FMX is configured for this full range on one platform, with a mobile app that works in the challenging connectivity conditions of large industrial sites.
