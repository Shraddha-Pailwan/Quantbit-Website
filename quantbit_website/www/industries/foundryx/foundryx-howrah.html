FAQ

Questions Foundry Owners Ask Before Implementing FoundryX

Specific questions from foundry owners and plant managers in this cluster.

How does FoundryX help Howrah foundries maintain BIS IS:1538 certification records?
FoundryX maintains heat-wise records of all parameters required for BIS IS:1538 sanitary casting certification: chemical composition per heat, hardness test results, dimensional inspection records, and visual inspection outcomes. These records are stored digitally and can be retrieved instantly for BIS audits. The system generates heat-wise test certificates in the format that BIS inspectors require — eliminating the paper register searches that currently take hours before inspections.
Can FoundryX generate Municipal Contract Material Test Reports for Howrah foundries?
Yes. FoundryX generates Material Test Reports (MTRs) and Certificates of Conformance (CoCs) at dispatch — linked to the specific production heat, raw material lot, and inspection records for each consignment. For municipal corporation contracts requiring full material traceability, this documentation is generated in the standard formats required by PWD, KMDA, and other procurement bodies.
Is FoundryX suitable for Howrah foundries producing multiple sanitary fitting variants like manhole covers, gully traps, and DI fittings?
Yes. FoundryX handles unlimited product variants with item-specific configurations: separate inspection templates for manhole covers vs gully traps vs DI fittings, separate BIS standard references per item, and separate dispatch document formats. Inventory is tracked by item, size, and grade — preventing the mixing of variants that creates municipal contract compliance failures.
How does FoundryX support Howrah foundries that produce both sanitary castings and general engineering castings?
FoundryX is metal and product-agnostic within a single instance. A Howrah foundry producing sanitary fittings alongside general engineering castings (machine parts, brackets, housings) manages both product lines in the same ERP — separate item masters, separate quality plans, and separate customer records — with a consolidated production dashboard for management.
What is the typical implementation timeline and cost for a Howrah foundry with 20–60 employees?
FoundryX runs on open-source ERPNext with no per-user licence fees. For a 20–60 employee Howrah foundry, implementation covering heat management, production, rejection analysis, BIS documentation, and dispatch typically takes 10–14 weeks at a cost of ₹2.5–5 lakhs including training and annual support.
