ERP Challenges Specific to Raipur Steel Casting Foundries

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

⚗️

Steel Grade Chemistry Not Tracked Per Heat

Raipur's alloy steel foundries add manganese, chromium, molybdenum, and other elements per heat — but track additions in paper records that cannot be retrieved when customers request chemistry certificates months later.

⛏️

Mining Wear Part Traceability Gap

Mining customers raise warranty claims for crusher liners and wear plates months post-delivery. Without heat-linked dispatch records, Raipur foundries cannot identify which heat produced the failed part or what chemistry and hardness it had.

💰

Steel Cost Variance Unknown Until Year-End

Alloy price volatility makes steel casting cost management critical. Most Raipur foundries discover actual vs standard cost variance only at year-end audit — too late to adjust quotations or procurement strategy.

📋

Inter-State E-Way Bill Generation Manual

Raipur's national-market customers require e-way bills for every consignment. Manual generation from dispatch registers is error-prone and creates delays at state border checkpoints that damage customer relationships.

Questions Foundry Owners Ask Before Implementing FoundryX

Specific questions from foundry owners and plant managers in this cluster.

How does FoundryX handle steel grade and alloy chemistry tracking for Raipur's alloy steel foundries?
FoundryX records the complete chemistry control record for each steel heat: charge composition (scrap grades, ferro-alloy additions by weight), in-furnace chemistry samples, ladle chemistry after tapping, deoxidation treatment, and final poured chemistry. Each heat gets a unique heat number linked to these records — so when a customer requests a material test certificate months after delivery, the heat chemistry is retrieved in seconds. The system also alerts when chemistry deviates from grade specification before the heat is poured.
Can FoundryX generate material test certificates for mining equipment castings produced in Raipur?
Yes. FoundryX generates Material Test Certificates (MTCs) at dispatch — linked to the production heat, showing chemical composition, hardness test results, and heat treatment parameters. For mining customers (SAIL, Coal India contractors, cement companies, quarry operators), these certificates are generated in the standard EN 10204 3.1 or 3.2 format as required by the customer specification.
How does FoundryX handle the inter-state e-way bill requirements for Raipur foundries supplying national mining companies?
FoundryX generates e-way bills automatically at the time of dispatch for all inter-state consignments — integrated with the GST e-way bill portal. Vehicle number, consignee GSTIN, HSN codes, and taxable values are pulled from the dispatch record automatically. This eliminates manual e-way bill preparation and reduces border checkpoint delays that affect Raipur-to-national-site deliveries.
Is FoundryX suitable for Raipur foundries producing both steel castings and grey iron castings in the same facility?
Yes. FoundryX handles multiple metal types and alloy grades within a single instance. A Raipur foundry running a steel induction furnace alongside a grey iron cupola can manage both metal streams in the same ERP — separate heat records, separate chemistry tracking parameters, separate inspection standards, and separate customer documents — with a unified production dashboard.
What is the implementation timeline and cost for a Raipur steel casting foundry with 30–80 employees?
FoundryX runs on open-source ERPNext with no per-user licence fees. For a 30–80 employee Raipur steel casting foundry implementing heat management, chemistry tracking, production, rejection analysis, and dispatch modules, typical implementation is 10–14 weeks at ₹3–6 lakhs including training and annual support.
