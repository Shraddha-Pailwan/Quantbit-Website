ERP Challenges Specific to Hyderabad Precision Foundries

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

Aerospace OEM Traceability Requirements Growing

Hyderabad foundries supplying aerospace OEMs face increasingly stringent traceability requirements — full forward and backward material traceability, heat-linked test records, and operator qualification records — that paper-based systems cannot provide.

📋

AS9100 Documentation Audit Preparation Takes Weeks

Preparing AS9100 surveillance audit documentation manually from paper records and multiple spreadsheets takes weeks of staff time. FoundryX maintains all required records in real time — audit readiness is continuous, not a sprint before each visit.

Alloy Chemistry and Heat Treatment Records Disconnected

Alloy steel and special alloy foundries track chemistry and heat treatment in separate lab spreadsheets. Without linkage to the production heat and dispatch record, material traceability is incomplete — a critical gap for aerospace customers.

🔍

NDE and CMM Results Not Linked to Heat Records

Dimensional and NDE inspection results stored in separate inspection department files. When a field failure occurs, linking it back to the exact casting lot, heat, and operator requires days of manual cross-referencing.

FAQ

Questions Foundry Owners Ask Before Implementing FoundryX

Specific questions from foundry owners and plant managers in this cluster.

How does FoundryX support Hyderabad foundries supplying aerospace customers with AS9100 requirements?
FoundryX maintains all records required for AS9100 compliance: first article inspection reports, in-process inspection records, material certificates linked to production heats, non-conformance reports with corrective action tracking, operator qualification records, and equipment calibration schedules. These records are stored in the ERP and can be retrieved for AS9100 surveillance audits instantly — replacing the weeks-long manual documentation preparation that currently precedes each audit.
Can FoundryX handle investment casting workflows for Hyderabad's precision casting sector?
Yes. FoundryX supports investment casting workflows: wax pattern consumption tracking, ceramic shell dipping records by layer, dewaxing and casting records, post-cast processing (heat treatment, HIP), and final inspection. Each investment casting lot is linked back through the entire process to raw material receipt — providing the full forward traceability required by aerospace and defence customers.
How does FoundryX integrate with CMM machines and NDE equipment used in Hyderabad precision foundries?
FoundryX integrates with CMM machines and NDE equipment through its API layer and import/export interfaces. Dimensional inspection results from CMM systems and NDE results (UT, RT, PT, MT) can be imported into FoundryX and linked to the production heat and lot number automatically. This creates a single quality record per casting lot that includes both the production data and the inspection results — the complete material traceability dossier that aerospace OEMs require.
Is FoundryX suitable for Hyderabad foundries working with alloy steel, stainless steel, and aluminium?
Yes. FoundryX is metal-agnostic. For alloy steel and stainless steel foundries in Hyderabad, it tracks alloy addition records, ladle chemistry analysis, heat treatment parameter records (temperature, time, atmosphere), hardness and tensile test results — all linked to the production heat number. For aluminium foundries, it handles degassing records, density index measurements, and die or permanent mould specific parameters.
Does FoundryX support NADCAP compliance documentation for Hyderabad aerospace casting units?
FoundryX maintains process documentation that supports NADCAP compliance for heat treatment (temperature uniformity survey records, AMS2770 compliance) and special process controls. The system records process parameters, operator qualifications, and equipment calibration data in the format required for NADCAP audits.
