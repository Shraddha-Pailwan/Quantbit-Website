Pain Points

Why Indian Foundries Struggle Without a Purpose-Built ERP

Generic ERP systems like SAP and Tally were not built for foundry operations. They miss the workflows that matter most to casting businesses.

🔥

No Digital Heat Records

Heat details recorded manually in registers — untraceable when a customer raises a rejection claim. No link between raw material used and castings dispatched.

📋

Pattern Register in Excel

Pattern location, condition, last-used date, cavity count, and repair history tracked in spreadsheets. Pattern goes missing, production stops.

❌

Rejection Without Root Cause

Rejection percentage known, but nobody knows which heat, which cavity, which shift, or which defect type is causing it. Pareto analysis done monthly, not daily.

🔧

Machining Subcontracting Chaos

Castings sent to machining vendors tracked in challan books. No system knows how much is with which vendor, what was returned, and what was rejected at machining.

💸

Unknown Cost Per Casting

Actual cost per casting — metal, power, labour, overhead — is calculated at month-end, not in real time. Quoting new parts is guesswork, eroding margins.

📦

Dispatch Without Traceability

Dispatch challan raised without linking to the heat or inspection record. OEM customers demanding material test reports and heat certificates — no system to generate them.

Have Questions About Foundry ERP Software?

Everything foundry owners, plant managers, and production heads commonly ask us about FoundryX.

What is FoundryX ERP software?
FoundryX is a purpose-built foundry ERP software developed by Quantbit Technologies on the ERPNext framework. Designed specifically for iron and steel foundries and casting companies in India, it manages the complete workflow from heat and melt management, pattern tracking, and production, to quality inspection, subcontracting, and dispatch with full heat-number traceability. Unlike generic ERP systems, FoundryX includes foundry-specific modules that handle mould production, sand management, grade-wise rejection tracking, and AI-powered quality inspection.
Which foundry clusters in India does FoundryX serve?
FoundryX serves foundries across all major Indian foundry clusters: Kolhapur and Pune (Maharashtra) for automotive castings, Belgaum (Karnataka) for precision automotive parts, Rajkot (Gujarat) for diesel engine castings, Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu) for pump-set castings, Batala and Jalandhar (Punjab) for agricultural machinery castings, and Howrah (West Bengal) for sanitary castings. Quantbit Technologies has offices in Kolhapur and Pune, providing direct on-site implementation and support to Maharashtra foundry clusters.
What is heat tracking in foundry ERP and why does it matter?
Heat tracking in foundry ERP is the digital recording of every melting heat — capturing the charge composition, furnace temperature, pour time, alloy grade, heat number, and the castings produced from that heat. FoundryX automatically assigns a unique heat number to every batch, linking it forward to casting, quality inspection, and dispatch records. This enables full traceability — if a customer reports a defect, you can trace it back to the exact heat, shift, operator, and raw materials used. Heat tracking is essential for automotive OEM suppliers and ISO 9001 / IATF 16949 compliance.
How does FoundryX handle rejection analysis compared to manual tracking?
FoundryX records every rejection against the heat number, cavity number, defect code (shrinkage, porosity, cold shut, blow hole, etc.), operator, shift, and mould. It generates automated Pareto analysis showing the top rejection causes, trend charts by heat or time period, and drill-down reports by customer part number. Quantbit clients typically see 35–40% reduction in rejection rates in the first quarter after implementing FoundryX because the system makes rejection patterns visible that were previously hidden in manual registers.
How long does foundry ERP implementation take?
A standard FoundryX implementation for a single-plant foundry takes 8–12 weeks, covering discovery, configuration, data migration, UAT, and go-live. Multi-plant or large foundries may take 16–20 weeks. Quantbit follows a phased approach — starting with core modules (heat management, production, inventory, dispatch) and adding quality, subcontracting, and finance in subsequent phases to minimise business disruption. Implementation is done on-site by our Kolhapur and Pune teams.
Is FoundryX better than iCast or VASY Cast ERP for Indian foundries?
FoundryX is built on ERPNext — a fully open-source platform — which means no per-user licence fees and no vendor lock-in, unlike iCast and VASY Cast which use proprietary systems. FoundryX includes AI-powered visual quality inspection (AlsensePro) which neither iCast nor VASY offer. It is GST-native and integrates HRMS, CRM, Helpdesk, and LMS in the same platform. FoundryX is developed and supported in India by a team based in Kolhapur — the heart of India's automotive foundry cluster — providing faster and more contextually relevant support than alternatives.
