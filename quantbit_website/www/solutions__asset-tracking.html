The problem

In most facilities, nobody can answer basic questions with confidence: how many assets do we have, where are they, what condition are they in, and what have we spent on them? Assets live in outdated spreadsheets or nobody's memory, so maintenance is guesswork, budgeting is fiction, and assets go missing or fail without warning.

Solve Asset Tracking & Visibility for Good
