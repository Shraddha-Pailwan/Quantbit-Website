Common questions

ERPNext Implementation — Answered

ERPNext is a full-featured, open-source ERP system built on the Frappe framework. It covers accounting, inventory, manufacturing, HR, CRM, projects, and more in a single integrated platform. Businesses choose ERPNext because it eliminates the high licence fees of SAP or Oracle while delivering comparable functionality — with the added benefit of complete customisability and a strong global community backing it.
A standard ERPNext implementation for an SME with 20–100 users typically takes 8–16 weeks from kickoff to go-live. The timeline depends on the number of modules, data complexity, customisation requirements, and your team's availability for UAT. Quantbit follows a structured 5-phase methodology that keeps projects on schedule.

Yes. Quantbit has migrated businesses from Tally, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, and various legacy systems to ERPNext. Our migration methodology includes data mapping, cleaning, validation, and parallel-run testing to ensure zero data loss and business continuity through the transition.
