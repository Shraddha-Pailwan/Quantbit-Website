Pain Points We Solve

ERP Challenges Common to Pune Foundries and Auto-Ancillary Units

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

🔥

Automotive OEM traceability failures

Pune OEMs demand heat-to-dispatch traceability on every shipment. Manual registers cannot produce this at audit time. FoundryX links heat number to casting to dispatch automatically.

❌

Rejection costing not captured

Rejection in precision casting is expensive. Without per-casting cost tracking, Pune foundries quote new parts without knowing actual rejection-adjusted margins.

🔧

In-house machining and outsourcing mixed

Many Pune units do partial machining in-house and send remainder to Bhosari subcontractors. Tracking material across both without a unified system creates inventory gaps.

📋

PPAP documentation every 6 months

Preparing PPAP submissions for OEM approvals manually takes weeks. FoundryX auto-generates the required reports from live production and inspection data.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions foundry owners ask us before implementing FoundryX.

Does FoundryX work for both foundry and machining operations at the same Pune plant?
Yes. FoundryX (for casting operations) and MachineX (for CNC and VMC machining) are both built on the same ERPNext platform by Quantbit. They share a single database — heat records from casting flow directly into machining job cards without duplication. This is unique to Quantbit and critical for Pune units that do integrated casting and machining.
Can FoundryX handle multi-plant operations across Bhosari, Chakan, and Ranjangaon?
Yes. FoundryX supports multi-plant / multi-warehouse configuration within a single ERPNext instance. You get consolidated dashboards for production, inventory, quality, and dispatch across all plants, with plant-wise breakdowns available at any time.
How does FoundryX help with automotive PPAP and IATF 16949 compliance in Pune?
FoundryX maintains all the records required for PPAP submissions: heat composition per batch, in-process inspection records, dimensional inspection data, hardness and tensile strength test results, and customer-specific quality plans. Reports are auto-generated — eliminating the manual compilation that takes weeks before an OEM audit.
What is the implementation timeline for a Pune foundry?
With our Pune HQ team, a standard Pune foundry implementation takes 8–10 weeks — faster than the industry average because our team is on-site from day one. We typically begin with the highest-ROI modules: heat management, rejection analysis, and dispatch, then add finance and HR in phase two.
Is FoundryX suitable for precision and investment casting units in Pune?
Yes. FoundryX handles ferrous (grey iron, SG iron, alloy steel) and investment/precision casting workflows. For investment casting, it tracks wax pattern consumption, ceramic shell dipping records, and heat-wise chemical composition — all linked to the final casting's quality and dispatch records.
