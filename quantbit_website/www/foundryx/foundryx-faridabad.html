FAQ

Questions Foundry Owners Ask Before Implementing FoundryX

Specific questions from foundry owners and plant managers in this cluster.

Does FoundryX support IATF 16949 documentation requirements for Faridabad auto casting suppliers to Maruti and Hero?
Yes. FoundryX generates and maintains all records required for IATF 16949 compliance: in-process inspection records, measurement system analysis data, corrective action records, process capability data, control plan linkage to production records, and management review data. For Maruti Suzuki and Hero MotoCorp supplier audits, the complete quality record for any production period is available instantly from the ERP — replacing the weeks-long manual compilation that currently precedes each customer audit.
How does FoundryX generate PPAP documentation for new part approvals at NCR automotive OEMs?
FoundryX maintains the production data required for PPAP Level 3 submissions: first article inspection results (dimensional, material, functional), process capability studies, initial samples records, appearance approval records, and control plan documentation. Part Submission Warrants (PSW) and the full PPAP documentation package are compiled from live ERP data — reducing PPAP preparation time from 2–3 weeks to 2–3 days.
Can FoundryX handle 50+ part numbers across multiple automotive customers simultaneously for Faridabad foundries?
Yes. FoundryX handles unlimited item variants with customer-specific configurations: separate inspection templates, drawing revision management, customer-specific defect code libraries, and part-specific control plans for each OEM. The production scheduling module handles multi-part, multi-customer production planning with tooling and raw material constraints — replacing the Excel-based planning that creates scheduling conflicts in high-mix NCR foundry operations.
How does FoundryX support traceability when Maruti or Honda raises a field warranty claim?
When an automotive OEM raises a warranty claim and requests casting traceability, FoundryX retrieves the complete production record for the specific casting — heat number, charge composition, pour temperature, cavity number, inspection results, operator, shift, and dispatch date — in under 30 seconds by searching on the part number, batch number, or invoice number. This 30-second response replaces the days of manual cross-referencing that currently delays NCR foundries' warranty investigations.
