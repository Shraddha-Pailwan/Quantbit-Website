Maintenance management

Preventive maintenance scheduled, breakdown calls tracked, service history preserved

HISx manages both planned preventive maintenance and unplanned breakdown maintenance in a single workflow — with every service event recorded against the asset's permanent history.

  • Preventive maintenance schedules auto-generated per asset — manufacturer intervals or custom biomedical team schedules
  • Breakdown calls logged instantly — downtime clock starts at call creation, ends when asset is returned to service
  • Full service history per asset — every PM, breakdown, parts replaced, engineer who attended, and time to resolution
  • Asset downtime reports — identify chronically unreliable equipment and build the data case for replacement

AMC / CMC management

Never let an AMC lapse — every contract tracked, every renewal alerted

AMC and CMC contracts for medical equipment are high-value, time-sensitive, and easy to miss when managed in spreadsheets. HISx tracks every contract, every service call against it, and alerts the biomedical team well before expiry.

  • AMC and CMC contracts maintained per asset with vendor, covered services, start/end dates, and contract value
  • Configurable renewal alerts — 90, 60, and 30 days before contract expiry — sent to biomedical head and purchase team
  • Service calls tracked against contract scope — out-of-contract calls flagged for separate vendor billing
  • Contract history per asset — full record of past AMC/CMC periods for renewal cost negotiation

All capabilities

Complete hospital asset management

From the asset register to the disposal entry — every asset management workflow in one platform.

Comprehensive asset register

Every asset — medical equipment, furniture, IT hardware, vehicles — in a single register with serial number, model, department, location, purchase date, supplier, and capitalised value.

Asset allocation & transfer

Asset allocation to departments, wards, and staff tracked with transfer history. Every reallocation documented with reason, authorised by, and date — no undocumented asset movements.

Depreciation tracking

Straight-line, written-down value, or units-of-production depreciation calculated and posted automatically. Asset book value maintained in real time. Finance gets depreciation schedules without manual calculation.

Asset downtime reporting

Downtime per asset, per department, per month — with mean time to repair and vendor SLA compliance. Evidence-based replacement decisions instead of gut feel.

Disposal & write-off

Asset disposal workflows with approval routing, sale proceeds posting, and write-off entries. Disposed assets removed from active register with full disposal documentation maintained in history.

Alerts & notifications

Configurable alerts for PM due dates, AMC/CMC expiry, warranty expiry, and asset downtime thresholds — delivered by push notification, SMS, or email to the right person at the right time.
