Why it matters

Three inventory problems that quietly drain hospital budgets

Manual inventory tracking creates invisible losses — in expired stock, unaccounted ward consumption, and stock figures that don't reflect reality until a physical count reveals the gap.

Expired stock written off — batch tracking is manual

Without batch-level tracking and near-expiry alerts, hospitals discover expired drugs only during physical counts. By then the write-off is unavoidable and the compliance exposure is already there.

Physical counts reveal gaps — but reconciliation takes weeks

Without structured cycle counting and variance workflows, physical audits are one-day events followed by weeks of spreadsheet reconciliation. Write-offs are guesses, not validated figures.

No traceability — auditors ask, nobody can answer

When an accreditation auditor or insurance investigator asks "show me every movement of Batch B-2024-031 from receipt to dispensing" — a system without barcode traceability has no credible answer.

Batch & expiry management

Every batch tracked from receipt to the last tablet dispensed

HISx tracks inventory at the batch level through the entire lifecycle — from GRN receipt through ward transfers, dispensing, returns, and write-offs. No batch is ever anonymous.

  • Batch-wise stock movement: every receipt, transfer, dispensing, and return linked to a specific batch and expiry date
  • Near-expiry alerts at configurable thresholds (30 / 60 / 90 days) — triggered at store, ward, and dispensing counter level
  • Batch-wise financial reporting including GST input/output, purchase GST values, and return and transfer taxation impact
  • Identical items procured at different rates tracked separately per batch — no averaging that distorts true cost

Barcode & traceability

End-to-end item traceability — from supplier delivery to patient dispensing

When an auditor, accreditor, or pharmacovigilance investigation asks about a specific batch or item, HISx provides a complete, timestamped chain of custody — scannable at every stage.

  • Barcode / QR scanning at GRN, dispensing, internal transfers, and physical stock audits
  • End-to-end item traceability: supplier → central store → ward → patient — every handoff recorded
  • Comprehensive audit logs for all stock movements, adjustments, user activities, and approvals
  • Recall management: identify all patient dispensings from a recalled batch instantly

Reorder & physical audit

Automated reorders and structured physical counts — inventory runs itself

HISx removes two of the most labour-intensive tasks in hospital inventory management: deciding when to reorder, and reconciling physical counts against system stock.

  • Automated reorder triggers at minimum / reorder / maximum stock levels — PR auto-generated when threshold is breached
  • Physical stock audit workflows with cycle counting — audit different store sections on a rolling schedule
  • Variance reporting: system quantity vs physical count, with item-wise and category-wise breakdowns
  • Approval-gated write-offs and shrinkage adjustments — no unilateral stock changes without authorisation trail
