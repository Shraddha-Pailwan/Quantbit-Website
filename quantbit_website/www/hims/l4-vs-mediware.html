The open-source advantage

Why hospital decision-makers choose ERPNext over proprietary systems

For growing hospitals and pharmacy chains, proprietary software creates three long-term problems that open-source avoids.

  • No platform licence fee — ERPNext is open-source. Total cost over 5 years is implementation, hosting, and support — not annual licences that compound
  • Any custom workflow buildable — need a ward-specific indent form? A custom NABH report? A CPOE integration? Any Frappe partner can build it without waiting for a vendor release cycle
  • No integration middleware costs — ERPNext's full REST API connects to HIS, LIMS, payment gateways, and government portals without expensive connectors
  • Multiple implementation partners — not locked to one vendor for support, upgrades, or future customisation
