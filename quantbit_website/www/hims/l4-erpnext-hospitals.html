What ERPNext covers for hospitals

ERPNext core — the foundation every hospital needs

Before any hospital-specific customisation, ERPNext provides a robust foundation covering the operational backbone of any hospital.

Procurement & vendor management

Multi-level approval PR → PO → GRN, vendor master with GST details, rate contract management, purchase returns, and demand forecasting — all built into ERPNext core.

Multi-location inventory

Stock management across central pharmacy, ward satellite stores, general stores, and lab — with inter-location transfers, reorder triggers, and real-time stock reports per location.

Accounting & GST compliance

Full double-entry accounting, GST-compliant billing and tax invoices, department-wise P&L, GSTR reports, and financial consolidation for hospital groups — no separate accounting system needed.

HR & payroll

Employee records, attendance, leave management, payroll processing, and staff expense claims — for clinical and administrative hospital staff, with department-wise cost allocation.

Asset management

Asset register, depreciation tracking, maintenance scheduling, and AMC/CMC contract management — ERPNext core covers the basics; HISx extends it with hospital-specific PM workflows and downtime tracking.

API & integration platform

Every ERPNext record and workflow is accessible via REST API. Connects to CPOE, LIMS, HIS, payment gateways, and government portals (GST, e-Way bill) without expensive middleware.

What HISx adds

HISx — the hospital-specific layer on ERPNext

ERPNext is a powerful general-purpose ERP. HISx adds the specialised modules and workflows that hospitals need and that ERPNext's core doesn't cover out of the box.

  • FEFO-enforced pharmacy dispensing with real-time stock visibility for clinical staff
  • Schedule H, H1, and NDPS narcotics compliance — double-signature, automated register, compliance reports
  • CPOE integration — prescription to dispensing to patient billing without re-entry
  • Lab reagent inventory — post-opening expiry, QC consumption, cost-per-test calculation
  • Ward stock transfers, digital indent workflows, and ward-vs-patient consumption reconciliation
  • SNOMED CT and NRCeS clinical data standards for diagnoses, procedures, and medicines
