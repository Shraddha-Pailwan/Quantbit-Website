Why it matters

Three problems every hospital pharmacy faces

Manual dispensing systems leave hospitals exposed to write-offs, patient safety risks, and billing inaccuracies every day.

Expired drugs dispensed — wastage and safety risk

Without FEFO enforcement, pharmacists pick whichever batch is within reach. Hospitals write off lakhs in expired stock annually and face the risk of expired drugs reaching patients.

Pharmacists are blind — doctors prescribe what isn't there

Without real-time stock visibility shared across roles, doctors prescribe medicines already out of stock at the ward level. Nurses chase the pharmacy. Patients wait.

Returns are a guessing game — inventory discrepancies pile up

Medicine returns processed on paper without restocking validation let unverified stock re-enter inventory. Billing reversals happen days later — if at all.

FEFO & expiry control

Zero expired drugs dispensed — FEFO enforced at every counter

FEFO (First-Expire, First-Out) is the dispensing discipline that ensures the earliest-expiry batch of any medicine is always used first. HISx enforces this automatically — the system selects the correct batch, not the pharmacist's memory.

  • Strict FEFO batch selection — earliest expiry always picked first, no manual override without authorisation
  • Near-expiry alerts at dispensing — configurable threshold (e.g. 30, 60, 90 days) triggers pharmacist notification
  • Batch-level dispensing controls with full audit log — every item tracked to its specific batch and expiry date
  • Expiry reports by item, batch, department — daily, weekly, and on-demand
