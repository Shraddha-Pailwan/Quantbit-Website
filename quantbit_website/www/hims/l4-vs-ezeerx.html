Frequently asked questions

Ezeerx and similar pharmacy point-of-sale systems are strong at counter-level billing and basic inventory for single-branch or small-chain pharmacies. ERPNext with HISx adds capabilities that growing pharmacy chains need: centralised procurement with vendor rate contracts, inter-branch stock transfers with batch tracking, consolidated reporting across all branches, FEFO enforcement at every branch counter, and full accounting integration including GST — all within one system rather than separate tools for each function.
A pharmacy chain typically outgrows point-solution systems like Ezeerx or WinRx when it reaches 3 or more branches, when centralised procurement decisions need to flow to branch-level POs, when inter-branch stock transfers become frequent enough to require a tracked system, when consolidated reporting across branches is needed without manual Excel consolidation, or when the chain's compliance requirements grow to include formal Schedule H logging and narcotics management. At that point, a full ERP like ERPNext with HISx provides the integrated platform the chain needs.

HISx on ERPNext covers counter-level pharmacy billing with GST-compliant tax invoices, day-end billing reconciliation, shift handover, discount management, and patient billing reports — the core billing functions that pharmacy POS systems provide. The difference is that ERPNext connects billing directly to procurement, inventory, accounting, and compliance within one system, rather than requiring a separate accounting or inventory tool alongside the POS.
Yes. HISx on ERPNext manages inter-branch stock transfers through a dispatch-and-receive workflow — the sending branch dispatches with batch and expiry details, the receiving branch confirms receipt, and both branch stock balances update automatically. In-transit stock is tracked separately until receipt is confirmed. FEFO is maintained across the transfer, and all movements are logged with batch numbers for compliance audit purposes.
