Why ERPNext for pharmacy

Three reasons pharmacy businesses choose ERPNext over proprietary software

Proprietary pharmacy software locks you into a vendor's roadmap, pricing, and integration constraints. ERPNext removes all three.

No licence fee — open-source core

ERPNext is fully open-source under the GNU GPL licence. No per-user licence fee, no annual subscription to the platform itself. You pay for implementation, hosting, and support — not for the right to run the software.

Fully extensible — any workflow, any custom field

Any report, workflow, integration, or custom field can be built on the Frappe framework without touching ERPNext's core. A certified Frappe partner can extend it to match your exact operational requirements.

API-first — connects to any HIS or LIMS

ERPNext exposes every record and workflow via a REST API. Integration with CPOE, HIS, LIMS, payment gateways, and government portals (GST, e-Way bill) works without expensive middleware.

What HISx adds to ERPNext

ERPNext doesn't include pharmacy-specific compliance — HISx does

ERPNext's core inventory and procurement is powerful, but it wasn't built for Indian pharmacy regulations. HISx adds the pharmacy-specific compliance and dispensing layer on top of ERPNext's strong foundation.

  • FEFO enforcement at dispensing — ERPNext tracks batch and expiry; HISx enforces FEFO at the dispensing counter automatically
  • Schedule H, H1, and NDPS compliance — prescription linkage, double-signature dispensing, and automated narcotics register
  • CPOE integration — prescription-to-dispensing-to-billing without re-entry at any stage
  • Day-end billing reconciliation, shift handover, and partial consumption billing
  • Barcode scanning at GRN and dispensing with end-to-end item traceability
