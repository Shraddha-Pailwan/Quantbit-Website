Built for your challenges

Three inventory problems specific to multi-specialty hospitals

A 200-bed multi-specialty hospital is not a large pharmacy — it is a network of departments, each with its own consumption patterns, stock requirements, and regulatory obligations.

No unified view — ICU, OT, and pharmacy each run their own stock

Without a connected system, ward stock is managed on paper or personal spreadsheets. The central pharmacy doesn't know what's consumed in ICU until a weekly count reveals the gap.

Expired drugs found in ward stock — FEFO not enforced department-by-department

FEFO failure is most common not in the central pharmacy but in ward satellite stocks — where informal stock rotation means older batches sit while newer batches are used first.

NDPS register maintained manually — double-signature not enforced under pressure

In a busy ICU or post-op recovery, the double-signature requirement for NDPS drugs is skipped under time pressure and backdated later. Without system enforcement, the audit risk is constant.

Ward stock management

Every ward gets what it needs — tracked from indent to consumption

HISx manages stock at the ward level through a digital indent workflow. Ward staff raise indents from their mobile or desktop; pharmacy fulfils and dispatches; stock balances update at both ends automatically.

  • Digital ward indents with approval workflow — no paper indent forms, no phone calls to pharmacy
  • Separate stock balances per ward, per satellite pharmacy, and central store — all visible in one dashboard
  • Ward consumption tracked by patient or by bulk issue — cost allocated per department accurately
  • Unconsumed ward stock returned to central pharmacy through validated return workflow
