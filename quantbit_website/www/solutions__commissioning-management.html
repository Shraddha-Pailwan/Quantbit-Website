The problem

Projects are won and lost in the final 5%. Commissioning and handover — checklists, system testing, punch lists, sign-offs — are often managed on scattered spreadsheets and verbal follow-ups, so the close-out drags on, items fall through the cracks, and the handover date that the client is counting on keeps slipping.

What is commissioning management in construction and EPC?

Commissioning management in construction and EPC is the disciplined close-out of a project — verifying each system through testing and checklists, driving the punch list of open items to closure, and confirming handover readiness with documentation. It addresses the final 5% of a project that causes a disproportionate share of delays and disputes.
