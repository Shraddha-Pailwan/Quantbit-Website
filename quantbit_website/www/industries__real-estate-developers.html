BuildX for Real Estate

For a real estate developer, margin is decided during construction, not at sale. A project that overruns its construction budget by even a few percent across multiple towers can erase the profit the sales team worked hard to earn. BuildX gives developers tight control over construction cost, budgeting, contractor billing and material — across towers, phases and multiple projects — plus clean snag and handover management so buyers take possession without endless follow-up.
