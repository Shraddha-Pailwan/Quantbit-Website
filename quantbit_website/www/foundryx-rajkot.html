Rajkot's Diesel Engine Facts

The Rajkot Foundry Cluster — India's Diesel Engine Casting Hub

Rajkot's foundry cluster developed primarily to serve the local diesel engine industry — Rajkot has historically been a major manufacturer of diesel engines and engine components used in agriculture, construction, and power generation. Today, the cluster's 500+ units produce a wide range of grey iron and ductile iron castings including engine blocks, cylinder heads, flywheels, brake drums, and pump bodies. The cluster also has a significant number of units producing castings for export, particularly to the Middle East and Africa.

ERP Challenges Specific to Rajkot Foundries

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

📋

Paper-based heat records for 10+ daily heats

Rajkot foundries running 10 heats per day accumulate thousands of paper heat records annually. Traceability queries take hours or days to answer manually.

❌

Rejection cost not tracked per casting

With thin margins on grey iron engine parts, untracked rejection directly impacts profitability. Without heat-wise rejection data, Rajkot foundries cannot identify whether the issue is raw material, process, or mould-related.

📦

Inventory of grey iron scrap and returns

Return castings from engine assembly customers mixed with new production inventory without a system to distinguish them — leading to quality mix-ups.

💸

Quotation without real cost data

Rajkot foundries quote new diesel engine parts based on gut feel rather than actual metal, power, and rejection-adjusted cost per kg — a margin erosion problem FoundryX solves.

Yes. FoundryX is designed for high-volume operations. It handles multiple heats per day with batch-wise heat number assignment, automatic charge sheet generation, and real-time dashboard updates. The system scales from a 20-tonne/day job shop to a 150-tonne/day continuous pouring operation without architectural changes.
How does FoundryX help Rajkot foundries with VASY Cast ERP or iCast ERP users considering switching?
FoundryX is built on open-source ERPNext — meaning no per-user licence fees and no vendor lock-in, unlike VASY Cast (proprietary, Ahmedabad) and iCast (proprietary, Coimbatore). FoundryX also includes AI visual quality inspection (AlsensePro) and integrated HRMS, CRM, and Helpdesk — modules that VASY and iCast do not offer in the same platform.
Can FoundryX manage both grey iron and ductile iron production in the same Rajkot facility?
Yes. FoundryX supports multiple metal grades and alloy specifications within the same instance. Grey iron GCI 200, GCI 250, SG iron, and other grades are configured as separate material types with grade-specific chemistry targets, inspection parameters, and customer quality plans.
