FMX Serving Jaipur Facility Teams

Jaipur — Rajasthan's capital and India's Pink City — has evolved from a heritage and tourism destination into a significant industrial and IT hub. The Mahindra World City SEZ houses major IT and manufacturing operations, the Sitapura and Vishwakarma industrial estates support gems processing, textile, chemical and engineering manufacturing, and the city's government building stock is among the largest in Rajasthan as the state capital. A growing healthcare sector — SMS Medical College Hospital, Fortis, Manipal and Narayana — and major educational campuses complete a diverse facility landscape. Jaipur's Smart City initiative is also driving technology adoption across public infrastructure. FMX gives Jaipur facility teams one platform for asset management, preventive maintenance, helpdesk and SLA monitoring — from IT campuses to government buildings to manufacturing plants.
