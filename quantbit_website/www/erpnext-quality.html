Overview

What is ERPNext Quality Management?

ERPNext Quality Management embeds quality control directly into your procurement, production, and delivery workflows — so quality is not a separate afterthought but an integrated gate at every material movement. The module supports Quality Inspection at Goods Receipt, during production in-process checks, and at final goods dispatch before delivery to customers. Inspection templates are fully configurable with pass/fail criteria, numeric tolerances, and visual check instructions — ensuring every inspector follows the same standard regardless of shift or location. Non-conformance reports capture rejected materials with root cause analysis, disposition decisions — return to supplier, rework, scrap, use-as-is — and link directly to purchase return or production rework orders. Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) tracks every quality problem through investigation, root cause identification, action assignment, and effectiveness verification with due dates and escalation. Quality Goals let you set measurable targets — incoming rejection rate, in-process defect percentage, customer complaint response time — and track actuals on the Quality Dashboard in real time. For Indian businesses, this supports BIS, FSSAI, and pharmaceutical GMP inspection requirements. For GCC businesses in Oman and UAE, the module supports ISO 9001 readiness and food safety HACCP documentation. Quantbit Technologies configures inspection checklists, acceptance criteria, CAPA workflows, and supplier quality rating calculations during implementation.

Best Practices

Best Practices for Quality Management in ERPNext

Quantbit ensures effective adoption from Day 1 with field-tested implementation standards.

🔬

Define Inspection Templates Before Go-live

Create item-wise or category-wise inspection templates with acceptance criteria before the first GRN. Inspections without standards are subjective and inconsistent.

🎯

Make Inspection Mandatory at GRN

Configure ERPNext to require quality inspection before a GRN can move stock to usable warehouse. Bypassing inspection at receipt leads to mixed stock and downstream problems.

📋

Every NCR Gets a CAPA

Institute a rule that every Non-Conformance Report must generate a CAPA — even for minor deviations. This builds a quality culture and provides audit trail for ISO certification.

🏆

Score Suppliers Quarterly

Run the Supplier Quality Scorecard quarterly and share results with your top suppliers. Visibility of their performance data drives supplier improvement better than complaints alone.

📅

Set CAPA Due Dates Firmly

Always set and enforce due dates on CAPA action items. Open-ended CAPAs become permanent fixtures — configure overdue alerts and escalation in ERPNext.

📊

Review Quality Goals Monthly

Hold a monthly quality review meeting with the Quality Dashboard. Trends in rejection rates and complaint frequency often predict production or supplier problems 2 to 4 weeks before they escalate.
