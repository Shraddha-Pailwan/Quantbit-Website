FMX Serving Delhi NCR Facility Teams

Delhi NCR — spanning Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad — is one of India's largest and most diverse facility management markets. Corporate campuses in Gurugram and Noida's Sector 62, central government buildings across Delhi, premier hospitals on both the Delhi and Gurugram sides, major retail and mall complexes, and a dense cluster of manufacturing and logistics facilities make the NCR region a high-demand market for professional facility management. Government organisations managing large building portfolios, corporate groups with multi-campus presence, and FM service companies operating across the NCR all face the challenge of managing facilities at scale with consistency and accountability. FMX brings this under control — one platform for assets, maintenance, helpdesk, SLAs and portfolio analytics across the entire NCR facility estate.
