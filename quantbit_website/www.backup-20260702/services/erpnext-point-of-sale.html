Overview

What is ERPNext Point of Sale (POS)?

ERPNext Point of Sale is a modern browser-based POS system that works on any tablet, laptop, or touchscreen counter — online or offline — without additional POS software licensing. The POS is built directly into ERPNext, meaning every sale at the counter instantly updates inventory levels, captures GST, records customer data, and creates the accounting entries in real time — with zero manual synchronisation between your billing software and back office. For retail businesses in India, ERPNext POS generates GST-compliant receipts with HSN codes, correct tax breakdowns, and optional e-invoice generation. UPI, credit card, cash, and loyalty points can be accepted in any combination on the same bill. Customer loyalty programs with points accumulation and redemption are built-in. Multi-outlet management lets head office see sales, inventory, and performance for every store from a central dashboard — eliminating the daily manual sales report consolidation that most retail chains still do. Offline mode ensures billing continues even when internet goes down — transactions queue and sync automatically when connectivity is restored. For restaurant and quick-service businesses, table-wise ordering, KOT printing, and split billing are available. For the GCC, ERPNext POS supports OMR, AED, and multi-currency billing, VAT-compliant receipts, and Arabic print formats. Quantbit Technologies configures POS profiles per outlet, payment modes, tax templates, customer groups, loyalty programs, and cashier shift management — ensuring your retail operations are live, compliant, and connected from Day 1.

Best Practices

Best Practices for Point of Sale (POS) in ERPNext

Quantbit ensures effective adoption from Day 1 with field-tested implementation standards.

📶

Test Offline Mode Before Go-live

Simulate an internet outage before going live and confirm offline billing and sync work correctly. Finding this gap during live operations is far more disruptive.

🔍

Barcode All Products at Launch

Invest in barcoding your product range before POS go-live. Manual item search during peak billing is slow and error-prone — barcodes reduce billing time by 60 to 80 percent.

📅

Daily Shift Closure Without Exception

Train cashiers to close every shift the same day. Unclosed shifts make the daily sales report inaccurate and create cash accountability gaps that compound over time.

🧾

Verify GST Template on POS Profile

Before billing the first customer, verify that the correct GST template is applied to the POS profile and that HSN codes are assigned to every item. An incorrect template on the first day is retroactively painful to fix.

🌟

Promote Loyalty at Counter

Train cashiers to capture the customer's mobile number at every billing opportunity. Loyalty data is your most valuable re-marketing asset — but only if you collect it systematically from day one.

📊

Weekly Outlet Comparison Review

Hold a weekly review of the outlet comparison report. Sales per square foot, average bill value, and return rates across outlets reveal training needs, product placement issues, and demand patterns quickly.

India & GCC Context

Compliance & Regional Considerations

ERPNext configured for India's statutory requirements and GCC regulatory frameworks out of the box.

🇮🇳 For Indian Businesses

ERPNext POS is fully compliant with India's GST requirements for retail. Every bill generates CGST and SGST for intra-state sales or IGST for inter-state supply. HSN codes are mandatory on items and print on every receipt. For businesses above the e-invoicing threshold, POS invoices for B2B customers can generate IRN and QR codes. Composition scheme retailers have a separate billing format. Daily GST collection by rate is available for GSTR-1 B2C section. UPI payment acceptance — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm — via QR or intent is configured through payment modes. State-wise professional tax configuration for store staff is linked to HR module.

🇮🇳 India — Key Configurations

  • CGST/SGST on intra-state, IGST on inter-state sales
  • HSN code mandatory on every receipt line
  • B2B POS e-invoice with IRN and QR code
  • Composition scheme billing format
  • GSTR-1 B2C daily sales summary
  • UPI payment: PhonePe, GPay, Paytm QR
  • Daily GST collection by rate for return filing
  • Barcode and weighing scale integration

🇪🇦 GCC / Oman — Key Configurations

  • VAT-compliant receipt with separate tax display
  • Multi-currency: OMR, AED, USD at counter
  • Arabic print format for customer receipts
  • Civil ID capture for regulated product sales
  • Loyalty program for Oman/UAE mall retailers
  • Muscat office for POS configuration and training
  • VAT daily collection summary for return
  • Multi-outlet management for GCC retail chains
