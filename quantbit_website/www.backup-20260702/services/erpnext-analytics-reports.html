Overview

What is ERPNext Analytics & Reports?

ERPNext is one of the most report-rich open-source ERP systems available, with over 200 standard reports covering every module — finance, sales, purchase, inventory, manufacturing, HR, and payroll. Unlike spreadsheet-based reporting where data must be manually compiled from multiple sources, ERPNext reports pull live data from the single transactional database — so every report reflects the current state of the business at the moment it is run.

Reports in ERPNext come in four types. Standard Reports are pre-built tabular reports that can be filtered, sorted, and exported to Excel or PDF. Query Reports are SQL-powered reports that Quantbit's team builds for complex cross-module analytics — such as customer profitability combining sales, purchase, and project data. Script Reports use Python to generate formatted outputs like financial statements and payroll registers with calculated totals. The Report Builder is a no-code interface where business users create their own custom tabular reports by selecting fields, setting filters, and choosing sort order — without any developer involvement.

ERPNext Dashboards allow you to create role-specific home pages with charts, number cards, and embedded report widgets. A Finance Director sees a dashboard with live cash balance, AR aging, AP due, and month-to-date P&L. A Production Manager sees work orders in progress, production vs plan, and WIP inventory value. A Sales Head sees monthly revenue, pipeline value, top 10 customers, and open quotation count — all refreshed without any manual data preparation.

Auto Email Reports schedule any ERPNext report to be delivered by email to defined recipients at a set frequency — daily sales summary, weekly inventory aging, monthly GST liability, or quarterly HR headcount. Reports can also be exported to Power BI, Tableau, Google Looker Studio, or any BI tool via ERPNext's REST API or direct database connection. Quantbit Technologies designs and builds custom reporting packages as part of every ERPNext implementation — delivering the specific insights your management team needs from day one.

Best Practices

ERPNext Reporting Best Practices

📊

Design Dashboards Before Go-Live

Identify the 5 to 8 KPIs each management stakeholder needs on their homepage before go-live. Dashboards designed at implementation are used daily. Dashboards designed after go-live are often never opened.

📄

Schedule the Weekly Pack on Day 1

Set up auto email reports for your weekly management information pack on the day of go-live. Automated delivery creates a habit — management receives and reviews data consistently rather than having to remember to log in and pull reports.

🔗

Use Report Builder for Ad Hoc — Script for Recurring

Train power users to use the Report Builder for ad hoc analysis. Reserve Script and Query Reports — which require developer involvement — for recurring, high-value reports that justify the build investment.

🔥

Restrict Report Permissions by Role

Configure report-level permissions so each role only sees reports relevant to their function. A sales executive should not have access to payroll reports, and a warehouse user should not see financial statements. Proper permissions increase data security and keep dashboards focused.

📋

Validate Standard Reports at UAT

During User Acceptance Testing, walk through the 10 most critical reports with business owners and validate the numbers against known data. Fixing report configurations before go-live avoids post-launch confusion and loss of trust in the system.

💌

Connect to Power BI for Board Presentations

If your board or investors expect polished visual dashboards, connect ERPNext to Power BI or Google Looker Studio. Quantbit builds the connector and data model — ERPNext remains the source of truth, BI tool handles the presentation layer.

Q: How do I automatically email a daily sales report to management from ERPNext?
ERPNext's Auto Email Report feature allows you to configure any report to be emailed automatically at a set schedule — daily, weekly, or monthly. You specify the report, recipients, frequency, and file format (Excel or PDF). The report is generated from live data and delivered to the recipient's inbox automatically — no manual intervention required.
