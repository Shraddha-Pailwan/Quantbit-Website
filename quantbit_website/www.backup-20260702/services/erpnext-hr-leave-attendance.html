Overview

What is ERPNext HR, Attendance & Leave?

ERPNext HR covers the full spectrum of human resource management from the moment an employee joins to the moment they leave — and everything in between. The Employee Master is a complete digital file capturing personal details, emergency contacts, educational and work history, documents, and custom fields specific to your organisation. Leave Management handles multiple leave types — casual leave, earned leave, sick leave, maternity, paternity, and optional holidays — with leave policies, allocation rules, encashment, and manager approval workflows. Attendance tracking integrates with biometric devices, manual entry, and mobile check-in with geo-fencing — giving HR teams accurate daily attendance data without spreadsheet consolidation. Shift Management supports rotational, fixed, and flexible shifts with assignment rules, swap requests, and overtime calculation. Expense Claims capture employee reimbursements with category-wise limits, manager approval, and direct integration with Accounts Payable for payment. Appraisal cycles allow structured performance reviews with goal setting, self-assessment, and manager ratings linked to HR records. For Indian businesses, ERPNext HR handles statutory requirements including PF, ESI, professional tax, and leave compliance under the Shops and Establishments Act. For GCC businesses in Oman and UAE, it supports visa expiry tracking, WPS integration for salary compliance, end-of-service benefit calculations, and Labour Law-aligned leave entitlements. Quantbit Technologies configures your complete HR setup — employee structure, leave policies, shift patterns, expense categories, and statutory deductions — during implementation.

Best Practices

Best Practices for HR, Attendance & Leave in ERPNext

Quantbit ensures effective adoption from Day 1 with field-tested implementation standards.

👥

Complete Employee Master at Joining

Capture all mandatory information including PAN, Aadhar, bank details, emergency contacts, and grade before the first payroll run. Incomplete masters cause payment and compliance failures.

📅

Define Leave Policy Before Allocation

Create leave types, policies, and carry-forward rules before the leave year begins. Retroactive policy changes cause leave balance discrepancies that are complex to correct.

🕐

Assign Shifts Monthly in Advance

Publish shift rosters at least one month ahead. Last-minute changes cause attendance errors, payroll discrepancies, and employee dissatisfaction.

🏢

Reconcile Biometric Data Weekly

Don't wait for month-end to reconcile biometric attendance. Weekly reconciliation catches device sync errors and manual corrections before they compound into payroll problems.

📋

Expense Policy Communication

Communicate expense category limits clearly to all staff. Configure ERPNext to enforce limits at submission — preventing the awkward post-approval reversal conversation.

📊

Monthly HR Dashboard Review

Review the HR Analytics dashboard monthly — headcount movement, attrition trends, leave liability, and absenteeism patterns often predict retention problems before they become critical.

India & GCC Context

Compliance & Regional Considerations

ERPNext configured for India's statutory requirements and GCC regulatory frameworks out of the box.

🇮🇳 For Indian Businesses

For Indian businesses, ERPNext HR covers all major statutory requirements. PF (Employees Provident Fund) contributions at 12% employer and employee are calculated from salary and sent to payroll. ESI deductions at 0.75% employee and 3.25% employer apply to eligible employees below the wage ceiling. Professional tax deduction varies by state — Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal — and is configured per state. Leave compliance follows the Shops and Establishments Act with state-specific mandatory leave types and accumulation rules. Gratuity eligibility calculation after five years of service is tracked from the joining date. Maternity leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act (26 weeks) is configured as a separate leave type.

🇮🇳 India — Key Configurations

  • PF 12% employer + 12% employee calculation
  • ESI deduction for eligible employees
  • State-wise professional tax configuration
  • Shops and Establishments Act leave types
  • Gratuity eligibility tracking from joining date
  • Maternity benefit: 26-week leave policy
  • LWF (Labour Welfare Fund) deduction
  • Form 16 input from salary and tax data

🇪🇦 GCC / Oman — Key Configurations

  • Visa and work permit expiry alert: 90/30 days
  • EOSB calculation per Oman/UAE Labour Law
  • WPS salary file generation for Oman/UAE
  • 30-day annual leave entitlement configuration
  • Air ticket allowance as leave or salary comp
  • Annual leave encashment workflow
  • Arabic employee documents and letters
  • Multi-nationality workforce management
