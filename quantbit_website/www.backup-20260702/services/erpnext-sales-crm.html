Overview

What is ERPNext Sales & CRM?

ERPNext Sales and CRM is a unified platform that connects your marketing leads, sales pipeline, quotations, sales orders, delivery, and invoicing into a single, unbroken workflow. Unlike standalone CRMs such as Zoho or Salesforce that require manual sync with your accounting system, ERPNext's Sales module sits natively inside the same database as your Inventory, Accounts, and Purchase modules — so a confirmed sales order instantly checks stock availability, triggers a delivery note, and auto-generates the invoice with zero re-entry. For Indian businesses, the module handles GST on quotations and invoices, HSN/SAC code assignment, e-way bill triggers for outward goods movement, and TCS deduction on high-value sales. For GCC businesses in Oman and UAE, it supports VAT-compliant invoicing in OMR and AED, Arabic print formats, and multi-currency pricing. The CRM features include lead capture from web forms and email, lead scoring and assignment rules, automated follow-up reminders, opportunity stage tracking with win/loss analysis, and sales team performance dashboards. Territory and target management lets you set monthly and quarterly targets per salesperson or region and track actuals in real time. Quantbit Technologies configures the full sales workflow — from lead sources and territory mapping to approval hierarchies for discounts and credit limits — ensuring your sales team can focus on selling rather than administrative data entry.

India & GCC Context

Compliance & Regional Considerations

ERPNext configured for India's statutory requirements and GCC regulatory frameworks — out of the box.

🇮🇳 For Indian Businesses

For Indian businesses, ERPNext Sales handles GST on every quotation and invoice based on the customer's billing state. HSN and SAC codes are assigned at item level and auto-applied on invoices. E-way bill triggers fire automatically for outward goods movement above the ₹50,000 threshold. E-invoicing via IRP generates IRN and QR codes for eligible businesses. TCS at 0.1% on sales above ₹50 lakh per customer is automated. Customer advances under GST are treated correctly with receipt vouchers. State-wise tax reporting for multi-state businesses is built-in.

🇮🇳 India — Key Configurations

  • GST auto-calculation on quotes and invoices
  • HSN/SAC code assignment and validation
  • E-way bill trigger for outward supply above ₹50K
  • E-invoicing via IRP with IRN and QR code
  • TCS on sales above ₹50 lakh threshold
  • State-wise GSTR-1 outward supply reporting
  • Customer advance under GST with receipt voucher
  • Multi-state GSTIN management

🇪🇦 GCC / Oman — Key Configurations

  • VAT-compliant invoicing for Oman, UAE, KSA, Qatar
  • Multi-currency pricing in OMR, AED, SAR, USD
  • Arabic print format for customer documents
  • VAT return data extraction per GCC country
  • B2B vs B2C supply type distinction at invoice level
  • Muscat office for GCC on-ground implementation
  • IFRS-aligned revenue recognition
  • Multi-currency AR aging and collections
