FAQs

Questions We Hear Before Every Project

How long does a typical ERPNext implementation take?
A standard single-company ERPNext implementation — covering finance, inventory, purchasing, and sales — typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from kick-off to go-live. Manufacturing modules with production planning, quality control, and shop floor integration typically take 10 to 16 weeks. The timeline depends primarily on the complexity of your existing processes and how quickly your team can participate in configuration reviews and user acceptance testing.
Can you migrate our data from Tally or SAP Business One?
Yes. We have run migrations from Tally Prime, Tally ERP 9, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, and several industry-specific legacy systems. The migration covers opening balances, customer and vendor masters, item masters, and historical transactions up to a cut-off date agreed during the scoping phase. All migrated data is validated against your source system before go-live.
Will customisations break when ERPNext upgrades?
Not if they are built the right way. Quantbit builds every customisation as a separate Frappe custom app using ERPNext's official hooks, override patterns, and custom doctype architecture. This means customisations live outside the core ERPNext codebase and survive version upgrades cleanly. When a major ERPNext release does change an underlying API that affects a custom app, we test and update the app as part of the upgrade engagement.
Do you support businesses outside India?
Yes. Quantbit has a physical office in Muscat and has delivered ERPNext implementations for businesses in Oman, UAE, and Kenya. Our implementation teams are experienced with Oman VAT, UAE VAT, and multi-currency setups. Support is available across both Indian Standard Time and Gulf Standard Time. For Kenya, we have experience with KRA-compliant invoice formats and multi-currency trading setups.
What does ERPNext managed services include?
Our managed services plan covers server monitoring and uptime management, daily automated backups with tested restore procedures, ERPNext version patching and security updates, performance tuning as data volumes grow, and a dedicated support channel for your team's day-to-day questions. It is designed for businesses that want ERPNext to run reliably without having to hire an in-house ERPNext administrator.
Can we start with one module and expand later?
Absolutely — and this is often the right approach for businesses that want to move quickly without overwhelming their team. Many of our clients start with Finance and Inventory, then add HR and Payroll in phase two, and integrate payment gateways or production planning in phase three. ERPNext's modular architecture makes phased rollouts natural, and our scoping process always maps out the full long-term picture so that phase-one decisions do not create obstacles for later phases.
