Overview

What is ERPNext Inventory & Stock?

ERPNext Inventory and Stock Management gives businesses complete visibility and control over every item across multiple warehouses, godowns, and locations — from the moment goods arrive at the dock to the moment they leave as a delivery or are consumed in production. The module supports FIFO, AVCO (Moving Average), and LIFO valuation methods with real-time stock ledger entries for every movement. Batch-wise and serial-number-wise tracking enables full traceability from purchase receipt to customer delivery — critical for pharmaceutical, food, electronics, and manufacturing companies subject to recall or warranty claims. Multi-warehouse management with sub-locations, bin-level tracking, and warehouse-wise P&L lets distribution businesses and multi-plant manufacturers see stock exactly where it sits. Reorder point configuration with automated purchase requests ensures stock-outs are prevented without human monitoring. Integration with ERPNext's Purchase, Sales, Manufacturing, and Quality modules means every material receipt, issue, delivery, and production job order automatically updates stock levels and valuation — no reconciliation needed. For Indian businesses, the module handles GST on stock transfers between states, e-way bill generation for inter-warehouse movement, and Schedule II goods tracking where required. For GCC businesses, it supports FEFO (First Expired First Out) for perishable goods management common in food and pharma distribution. Quantbit Technologies implements and configures the full inventory setup including item master, warehouse hierarchy, valuation method selection, reorder rules, and opening stock migration during go-live.

Best Practices

Best Practices for Inventory & Stock in ERPNext

Quantbit follows implementation standards that ensure effective adoption from Day 1.

🏭

Define Item Master Completely

Capture UOM, HSN code, valuation method, reorder level, and supplier info on each item before go-live. Incomplete masters cause downstream errors in purchase and production.

📦

Set Warehouse Hierarchy Correctly

Map your physical warehouse structure — warehouse, zone, rack, bin — in ERPNext before the first GRN. This enables precise location-level tracking from day one.

📅

Choose Valuation Method Once

Select FIFO or AVCO at item level before posting any stock entries. Changing valuation method after transactions requires complex revaluation and audit adjustments.

🔄

Enable Quality Inspection at GRN

Require quality inspection before stock acceptance for critical items. This prevents defective goods from entering production or being delivered to customers.

✊

Mandatory Batch for Perishables

Configure batch tracking as mandatory for all perishable items. Without it, FEFO picking cannot be enforced and expiry management becomes manual and error-prone.

📋

Cycle Count Instead of Annual Audit

Set up monthly cycle counting by ABC classification rather than waiting for one annual physical count. High-value A items should be counted monthly, B quarterly, C annually.

Compliance & Regional Considerations

ERPNext configured for India's statutory requirements and GCC regulatory frameworks — out of the box.

🇮🇳 For Indian Businesses

For Indian businesses, ERPNext Inventory handles GST on inter-state stock transfers with the correct IGST application and e-way bill generation for transfers above ₹50,000. Branch transfer pricing and GST treatment for consignment warehouses is configured during implementation. Schedule II goods with regulatory tracking requirements — such as pharmaceutical items — are handled with batch and serial controls. Import GRN includes customs duty and freight in the landed cost calculation for accurate COGS. E-way bill generation is integrated for outward movements above the threshold.

Q: How does ERPNext handle expiry date tracking for pharma distributors?
