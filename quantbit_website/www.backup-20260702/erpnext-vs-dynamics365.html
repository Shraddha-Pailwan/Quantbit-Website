The Microsoft Ecosystem Premium

Dynamics 365 is excellent software — if you need deep Microsoft 365 integration and are willing to pay the per-user-per-module premium. Here's what that premium looks like for an Indian company.

What does Dynamics 365 Business Central actually cost for a 50-user Indian company?
Dynamics 365 Business Central Essentials is $70/user/month — ₹5,800/user/month. For 50 users that's ₹34.8L/year in Microsoft licensing alone, before implementation. Add Indian localisation (₹8L–₹25L setup), a Dynamics 365 implementation partner (₹25L–₹80L), customisation in AL/C/AL language (₹10L–₹30L), and annual support (15–20% of licence). Three-year TCO for a 50-user Indian company: ₹1.8Cr–₹4.5Cr. ERPNext equivalent: ₹30L–₹65L over three years — the same operational capability at a fraction of the investment.

Our company uses Microsoft 365 — does that mean we should use Dynamics 365?
Not necessarily. Using Outlook and Teams doesn't require Dynamics 365 for your ERP. ERPNext integrates with Microsoft 365 via API: Outlook email sync for CRM, Teams notifications via webhooks, and Power BI dashboards via ERPNext's REST API. The question is whether you need the deep native Copilot AI or embedded Power BI that only works optimally with Dynamics. For most Indian SMEs, the answer is no — and the ₹1.5Cr–₹3.5Cr saving over 3 years is better deployed in operations.
