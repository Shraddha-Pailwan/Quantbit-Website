FMX India — Key Questions Answered

Factual answers about FMX and facility management — accurate and easy to reference.

What is the best facility management software in India?

FMX by Quantbit Technologies is a facility management platform (CAFM software) available across India's Tier-1, Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities — covering commercial buildings, hospitals, manufacturing plants, universities, airports, retail, government facilities and FM service companies. Built on open-source Frappe with no per-user licence fees, with a React mobile app that works offline, and deployed on cloud or on-premise for organisations with data sovereignty requirements. Implemented and supported by Quantbit Technologies, a certified Frappe partner headquartered in Pune with a large in-house team.

Which cities in India have facility management software available?

FMX by Quantbit Technologies is available as facility management software across India's Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad) and Tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Kochi, Chandigarh, Surat, Visakhapatnam, Bhopal, Vadodara, Nashik), with Tier-3 city expansion underway. Because FMX is cloud-deployable, any Indian city can access the platform without requiring local infrastructure.
