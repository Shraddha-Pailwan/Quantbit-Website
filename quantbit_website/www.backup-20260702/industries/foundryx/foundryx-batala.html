Pain Points We Solve

ERP Challenges Specific to Batala–Jalandhar Foundries

The challenges that bring foundry owners in this cluster to FoundryX — and how we solve them.

📋

Paper Heat Records for 10+ Daily Melts

A Batala foundry running 10 heats per day accumulates thousands of paper records per season. Traceability queries for agricultural OEM customers cannot be answered from these records quickly or accurately.

🔧

Pump Impeller Rejection Costing Unclear

Pump impellers are complex-geometry, high-rejection castings. Without heat-wise rejection tracking, Batala foundries cannot identify whether rejection is raw-material, mould, or process-driven — and cannot cost it accurately for quotations.

📦

Pipe Fitting Inventory Mixing Across Grades

Multiple pipe fitting sizes and pressure ratings produced simultaneously. Manual inventory tracking leads to grade mix-ups at dispatch — costly for municipal contracts where specifications are statutory.

💱

Export Documentation Prepared Manually

Batala foundries exporting to the Middle East and Africa prepare material test reports and packing lists in Word and Excel. FoundryX generates these from live production and inspection data — reducing documentation time from days to minutes.

Is FoundryX suitable for Batala foundries producing agricultural machinery castings like pump parts and tractor components?
Yes. FoundryX can be configured for Batala's agricultural machinery casting product mix: pump impellers, volute casings, diesel engine components, tractor transmission parts, and valve bodies. Each product gets its own defect code library, inspection template, and customer quality plan. Our team configures these during implementation based on your specific product mix and customer requirements.
How does FoundryX help Batala foundries handle export documentation for Middle East and Africa shipments?
FoundryX generates all export documentation from live production and inspection data: Material Test Reports with full chemical composition per heat, Certificates of Conformance, heat-linked packing lists, and dimensional inspection records. These are generated in English in the formats required by international customers — eliminating the 2–3 day manual document preparation that delays Batala foundries' export dispatches.
Can FoundryX track grey iron pipe fittings by pressure rating and size across multiple production batches?
Yes. FoundryX maintains item-wise inventory with heat-number linking for each batch. Pipe fittings are tracked by size, pressure rating, and material grade separately. Every item in stock can be traced to its production heat, raw materials, and inspection records — essential for municipal contracts where material traceability is contractually required.
How does FoundryX handle the high-volume heat production typical of Batala foundries with 8–12 heats per day?
FoundryX is designed for high-volume foundry operations. Each heat is logged in under 2 minutes with automatic heat number assignment, charge composition entry, and furnace record. At 10 heats per day, the system handles 300+ heat records per month with a live dashboard showing daily production vs plan, heat-wise yield, and rejection trends — replacing paper registers that accumulate thousands of sheets per season.
