The NetSuite Pricing Reality

NetSuite's "cloud ERP" label makes it sound accessible. The pricing model tells a different story.

⚠️ NetSuite Pricing Alert

NetSuite charges a platform fee ($999+/month), per-user fees ($99/user/month base), and separate module add-ons for Manufacturing, HR, Payroll, and Advanced Financials. India GST compliance requires SuiteApps (additional cost). For a 50-user Indian manufacturing company, total NetSuite cost exceeds ₹80L–₹1.5Cr per year in licensing before a single line of implementation work begins.

What does Oracle NetSuite actually cost for an Indian company with 50 users?
NetSuite pricing for 50 users with Manufacturing, HR, and Financials: platform fee $999/month + 50 users × $99/month + manufacturing module + HR module = approximately $8,000–$15,000/month, or ₹6.6L–₹12.4L/month. That's ₹80L–₹1.5Cr/year in licensing alone. India GST (SuiteApp) and India payroll add further cost. Implementation by a NetSuite partner in India runs ₹40L–₹1.5Cr. ERPNext for the same scope: ₹3L–₹8L/year subscription plus ₹12L–₹25L implementation — 75–85% lower total cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ERPNext a good alternative to Oracle NetSuite for Indian companies? +
Yes. For Indian SMEs and mid-market companies (₹20Cr–₹600Cr revenue, up to 500 users), ERPNext delivers 90%+ of NetSuite's functional capability at 65–80% lower cost. ERPNext's native GST, India payroll, and GCC VAT localisation outperform NetSuite's localisation at a fraction of the price.
What does Oracle NetSuite actually cost for an Indian company? +
NetSuite for 50 users with Manufacturing, HR, and Financials: approximately ₹80L–₹1.5Cr/year in licensing alone. Add implementation (₹40L–₹1.5Cr), India GST SuiteApp, and India payroll connector. Three-year TCO: ₹3Cr–₹7Cr+. ERPNext equivalent: ₹30L–₹71L over three years.
Does ERPNext have the same multi-subsidiary features as NetSuite? +
ERPNext supports multi-company natively with inter-company transactions and consolidated financials. NetSuite OneWorld is more advanced for very large global groups (50+ entities, 30+ countries). For Indian groups with 2–10 entities, ERPNext's multi-company handles the requirement fully.
Is NetSuite better than ERPNext for SaaS companies? +
NetSuite has historically been strong for SaaS companies needing US GAAP ASC 606 revenue recognition and SaaS metrics. ERPNext covers project accounting and billing for most Indian SaaS companies well. For startups scaling toward US/EU markets with investor-mandated SOX compliance, NetSuite has an edge. For the vast majority of Indian SaaS companies, ERPNext is more than sufficient at far lower cost.
Can I migrate from NetSuite to ERPNext? +
Yes. Quantbit migrates companies from NetSuite to ERPNext using NetSuite's CSV export or SuiteTalk API. Migration covers Chart of Accounts, customer/supplier masters, item masters, opening balances, and outstanding AR/AP. NetSuite SuiteScript customisations are rebuilt in Frappe. Timeline: 8–16 weeks depending on scope.
